About the event

Jazzaldia, the San Sebastián Jazz Festival, is one of Europe's oldest continuous jazz festivals, running in the Basque seaside city since 1966. The official 2027 edition is listed for 21–25 July 2027, with the festival remaining anchored in San Sebastián.
