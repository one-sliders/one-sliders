About the event

Nine concerts across four evenings, 4 to 7 November 2026, in the festival's thirtieth year. Six are at the National Theatre and two at the Youth Theatre, with the Macedonian National Jazz Orchestra opening and Faraj Suleiman's trio closing the Saturday at 22:00. Music Meeting, the festival's programme for young musicians from Bosnia and Herzegovina and the rest of Europe, runs alongside the concerts.

Nine concerts over four evenings, 4 to 7 November 2026, in the festival's thirtieth year. The bill runs from the Macedonian National Jazz Orchestra on the opening night through Alfredo Rodriguez with Pedrito Martinez, Patricia Brennan's septet and Harold Lopez-Nussa to Faraj Suleiman closing the Saturday. Music Meeting, the festival's programme for young musicians, runs alongside.

Both houses are seated. Six of the nine concerts are at the National Theatre, including every 22:00 late slot, and two are at the Youth Theatre - Dobrila & Dorian on the Friday and the Obradovic-Tixier Duo on the Saturday. On both Friday and Saturday the 20:30 concert at the Youth Theatre sits between two at the National Theatre, so moving between them is part of the evening.
