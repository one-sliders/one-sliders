About the event

Megadeth play Oslo Spektrum on 28 March 2027, in an arena holding about 10,000 seated and up to 11,500 standing.

Megadeth were formed in Los Angeles in 1983 by Dave Mustaine, shortly after he was fired from Metallica, and he has been the only constant member since. Five of their seventeen studio albums are certified platinum in the United States, and the 2026 self-titled album became their first to top the Billboard 200 — and, Mustaine has said, their last.
