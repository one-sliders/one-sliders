About the event

Iron Maiden's 'Run for Your Lives' tour marks the band's 50th anniversary with a set heavy on 1980s classics. The Yokohama show at K-Arena brings Bruce Dickinson, the towering Eddie props and pyrotechnics to Japan, long one of Maiden's most devoted markets.

Iron Maiden were formed in Leyton, East London, in 1975 by bassist and principal songwriter Steve Harris. They have sold more than 130 million albums worldwide; The Number of the Beast (1982) alone accounts for almost 20 million of them.
