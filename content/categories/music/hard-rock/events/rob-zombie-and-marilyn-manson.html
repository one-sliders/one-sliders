About the event

Rob Zombie and Marilyn Manson co-headline the Freaks on Parade tour, a theatrical, industrial-metal spectacle pairing two of shock rock's biggest names. Combining Zombie's horror-movie visuals with Manson's provocative stagecraft, it is one of the loudest and most visually extreme tours on the road.

Rob Zombie, born Robert Bartleh Cummings in Haverhill, Massachusetts, in 1965, fronted White Zombie until the band split in 1998 and began a solo career the same year. His debut Hellbilly Deluxe has sold over three million copies.
