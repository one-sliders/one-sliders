About the event

Inferno Metal Festival takes over Rockefeller and the clubs around it in Oslo for four days over Easter, and has done since 2001. The 2027 edition runs from 25 to 28 March with Carcass, Mgla, Arcturus, Blasphemy and Kampfar among the acts confirmed so far. A four-day pass is 4,495 kroner, and a three-day industry conference runs alongside the concerts.

The 2027 bill is already deep: Carcass, Mgla, Arcturus, Blasphemy, Kampfar, Master's Hammer, Green Carnation, The Ruins of Beverast, Hellripper, Rotten Sound and Asagraum are among the acts confirmed so far, with announcements continuing through the autumn. Around the concerts the festival runs a three-day conference for metal professionals and media, a tattoo fair and a metal market, which is why the Easter week in Oslo draws people who are not only there for the gigs.
