About the event

Prague Spring runs through May 2027, and two dates are already fixed: the Czech Philharmonic's calendar lists festival concerts in the Rudolfinum's Dvorak Hall on Sunday 16 May and Friday 21 May, both at 19:30. The festival itself announces the programme, the performers and the remaining venues in November 2026, and tickets are sold by the festival rather than the orchestra.

Prague Spring fills May 2027, and two dates are already fixed in the Rudolfinum's Dvorak Hall: Sunday 16 May and Friday 21 May, both at 19:30. The hall is the Czech Philharmonic's home, and the festival has opened on 12 May with Smetana's Ma vlast for decades. Who conducts, and what else is played, comes with the programme in November 2026.

Tickets are sold by the festival rather than by the orchestra, even for the Rudolfinum concerts - the Czech Philharmonic's own page says so and points to festival.cz. They can also be bought in person at the Rudolfinum box office. Sale dates follow the programme announcement in November 2026.
