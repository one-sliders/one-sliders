About the event

Lollapalooza Berlin is scheduled for 17 and 18 July 2027 at Olympiastadion and Olympiapark Berlin. The 2027 edition keeps the festival in Berlin, so the planning location remains the Olympic Park area rather than a new host city.

Stay in Berlin

Check-in 16 Jul, check-out 19 Jul 2027 — 3 nights around 17 July 2027 – 18 July 2027. Change the dates to stay longer.
