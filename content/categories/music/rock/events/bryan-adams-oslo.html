About the event

Bryan Adams plays Oslo Spektrum in spring 2027. The venue lists the concert on 3 May 2027 while the artist's own tour page gives 5 May; the date is not settled until tickets go on sale, and the booking window here follows the venue listing.

Bryan Adams was born in Kingston, Ontario, in 1959 and raised in Ottawa. Reckless (1984) made him a stadium act and became the first album by a Canadian to be certified diamond at home; seven years later “(Everything I Do) I Do It for You” spent sixteen weeks at number one in the UK. He has sold between 75 and more than 100 million records.
