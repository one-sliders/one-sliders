About the event

Rock am Ring runs 4 to 6 June 2027 at the Nurburgring, in parallel with its twin Rock im Park at the Zeppelinfeld in Nuremberg - the same bands play both, on different days. blink-182, Die Toten Hosen and Bring Me The Horizon headline, with Faith No More, Parkway Drive, Evanescence, Turnstile, Billy Talent and The Prodigy on a bill that had grown to around fifty names. More than 140,000 weekend tickets have gone across the two sites; Rock am Ring sells weekend tickets only.

Rock am Ring is one of Germany's largest rock festivals, staged at the Nürburgring motor-racing circuit in the Eifel hills since 1985, running a twin bill with Rock im Park over the same weekend.
