About this event

Mawazine, held across multiple outdoor stages in Rabat since 2001, is one of the largest music festivals in Africa by attendance, mixing major international pop and Arabic-language headliners with Moroccan and African artists. Its scale has made it a significant draw for global stars performing in North Africa.
