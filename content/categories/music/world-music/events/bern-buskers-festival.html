About the event

Buskers Bern is on 12 to 14 August 2027, the 24th edition. It is a street festival rather than a stage festival: the acts play in the lanes and on the squares of Bern's old town, and they are drawn from an application round that opens in November. The programme for 2027 is published later - the dates and the application window come first, and the line-up follows in the spring.

The 24th Buskers Bern runs 12 to 14 August 2027, and it has no main stage: in 2024 the festival counted 144 artists in 41 groups from 25 nations, playing some 300 separate shows in the lanes and squares of the old town. You walk between performances rather than queue for one. The 2027 line-up comes from an application round that opens in November.

The acts are paid in hat money, not fees - that is the festival's model, and it shapes the programme: concerts, street performance, theatre, dance, puppetry, acrobatics and art installations rather than a bill of touring names. The area is free to walk; three locations check the Baendeli wristband, which costs 20 francs and funds the festival.
