About the event

The Rainforest World Music Festival is a celebrated world-music gathering held in the Borneo rainforest near Kuching, in Malaysian Sarawak. Combining evening main-stage concerts with daytime workshops in a jungle setting, it brings together traditional and contemporary musicians from around the globe.
