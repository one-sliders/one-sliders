About the event

Oslo World is Norway's world music festival: 45 concerts in 14 Oslo clubs over six nights, 27 October to 1 November 2026. Marcel Khalife opens, Jovanotti closes, and in between come Egyptian zar, Colombian cumbia and Tuareg rock. Founded 1994. Tickets per concert.

Oslo World books 60 concerts and club nights from 35 countries into ten rooms across the city, from the 1,750-capacity Sentrum Scene down to Kafe Haerverk. The 2026 edition opens with Marcel Khalife at Chateau Neuf and closes with Jovanotti at Sentrum Scene on 1 November. In between the programme runs from Dubioza Kolektiv and Frente Cumbiero on the first night to Lido Pimienta, BCUC, Elida Almeida and Nai Barghouti, most of them playing a single night only.

Mazaher: Egyptian zar, a healing ritual led by women. BCUC: drum-driven psychedelia from Soweto. Frente Cumbiero: Bogotá cumbia. Imarhan: Tuareg rock from Algeria. Nai Barghouti: Palestinian voice with Norwegian jazz piano. Jovanotti: Italy's biggest pop star, the one arena-scale name.
