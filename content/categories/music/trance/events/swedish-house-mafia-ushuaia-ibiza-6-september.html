About the event

6 September is the penultimate Sunday of Swedish House Mafia's 2026 Ushuaia Ibiza residency, one week before the run ends on 13 September. September dates catch the island as the season winds down, and the show keeps the residency's open-air, 17:00-to-23:00 format on the Playa d'en Bossa stage with Bea Sylvan and Nosefine supporting.
