About this event

Tomorrowland, held on the Recreation Area De Schorre grounds in Boom since 2005, has grown from a local Belgian festival into one of the largest and most internationally attended electronic music events in the world, known for elaborate main-stage set design that changes completely each year and a global audience that regularly sells out within minutes.
