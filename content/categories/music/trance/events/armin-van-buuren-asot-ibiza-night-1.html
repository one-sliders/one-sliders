About this event

7 September opens Armin van Buuren's five-week Monday residency at UNVRS in San Rafael, a run built around the 25th anniversary of A State of Trance. Each night is an extended five-hour set rather than a standard club slot, and every show includes a vinyl-only hour - a format he has not previously built an Ibiza residency around. Handpicked guests join him week to week.
