About this event

Swedish House Mafia hold a Sunday residency at Ushuaia Ibiza across the 2026 season, playing the open-air stage on Playa d'en Bossa every Sunday from 28 June to 13 September. Twelve dates make this their longest run at the venue - the trio's previous Ushuaia residency, in 2024, lasted six weeks. Sets run in daylight and through sunset rather than late into the night, with doors from 17:00 to 23:00, and Bea Sylvan and Nosefine play alongside them each week.
