About this event

Melodifestivalen selects Sweden's Eurovision entry and has run since 1959, growing into one of the most-watched programmes on Swedish television. Heats tour arenas around the country before a final in Stockholm, and the competition has served as a launchpad for a large share of Sweden's pop export industry.
