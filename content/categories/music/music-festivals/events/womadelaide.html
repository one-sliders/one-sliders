About the event

WOMADelaide — World Of Music Arts & Dance in Adelaide — is an open-air festival in Botanic Park / Tainmuntilla, scheduled for 5–8 March 2027. The festival has been part of Adelaide’s cultural calendar since 1992 and presents music alongside dance, arts, food and ideas, giving the programme a deliberately wide view of cultural discovery. It is held on the traditional lands of the Kaurna People, a fact the organiser acknowledges in its presentation of the site. Across four days and nights, the festival pairs performance stages with activities such as talks and food programming rather than treating the grounds merely as a concert field. The organiser has also stated that its 2026 stages were powered with 100% renewable energy, part of its sustainability work. The 2027 dates are confirmed; return to the official festival programme for artists, performance times and any access details before making travel plans.
