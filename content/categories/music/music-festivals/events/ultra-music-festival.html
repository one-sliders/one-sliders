About Ultra Music Festival

Ultra Music Festival is one of the world's leading electronic music festivals, held each March in downtown Miami as the centrepiece of Miami Music Week. Its waterfront setting and A-list DJ line-ups have made it a flagship of global dance-music culture.

Next editionUltra Music Festival

The next Ultra Music Festival runs over three days in March in Miami. Multiple stages, including the landmark main stage, host the biggest names in electronic music against the backdrop of the city's waterfront.
