About the festival

Coachella is one of the most influential music festivals in the world, held over two weekends each April at the Empire Polo Club in Indio, California. As famous for its fashion, art installations and celebrity crowd as for its genre-spanning headliners, it sets trends far beyond the desert.

Travel tips
  • The two weekends have identical lineups — weekend two passes are usually easier to get.
  • Palm Springs (PSP) is the closest airport; shuttles run from Indio and Palm Desert hotel hubs.
  • Desert nights get cold — pack for 35°C days and 10°C nights.
