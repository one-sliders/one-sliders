About the event

Five days on a hill above the city: the 44th Gurtenfestival runs 14 to 18 July 2027 on the Gurten, reached by funicular from Bern. The setting is the point - the stages sit on a meadow with the Alps behind them rather than on an industrial site. Early-bird tickets are on sale before the line-up is announced.
