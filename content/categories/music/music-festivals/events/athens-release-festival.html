About the event

Release Athens is a series of concert nights rather than one weekend, and two of the 2027 dates are set: The Neighbourhood at the Markopoulo Complex on 25 June, and Tokio Hotel at ERGOSTASIO35 on 7 July. Ticket sales for the first opened on 1 October and are already running for the second. The organisers say more names follow.

Release Athens is a series of separate concert nights rather than one weekend, and two are set for 2027: The Neighbourhood at the Markopoulo Complex on 25 June, and Tokio Hotel at ERGOSTASIO35 on 7 July. You travel for a night, not a camp - and the organisers say more names follow.

Nothing clashes, because each date is its own night at its own venue: 25 June 2027 at the Markopoulo Complex, 7 July 2027 at ERGOSTASIO35. That is the structural difference from a camping festival - the only scheduling question is which nights to fly in for, and more are still to be announced.
