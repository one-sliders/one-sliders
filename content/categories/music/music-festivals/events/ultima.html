About the event

Ultima is Scandinavia's largest contemporary music festival, founded in 1990 and held across Oslo each September. The programme moves between the Norwegian National Opera, Oslo Concert Hall and the University's Great Hall and small clubs, shops, industrial spaces, museums and outdoor sites. The 2027 edition runs from 16 to 25 September, ten days in all.

Ultima does not sell a festival pass. Tickets are sold per event and usually direct from the venue that hosts it, with the link on each event's own page, so an evening with two concerts in different rooms means two separate purchases. That also means clashes are resolved by the buyer rather than by a wristband: there is no single queue, no single price and no transfer between rooms.
