About World Ranger Day

World Ranger Day honours the park rangers who protect the world's natural and cultural heritage - often in dangerous conditions - and commemorates those killed or injured in the line of duty. It is organised each year by the International Ranger Federation.

Next editionWorld Ranger Day

The next World Ranger Day is marked on 31 July with tributes, fundraising and events for rangers worldwide. It recognises their frontline role in protecting wildlife and wild places, and the risks they face.

How to take part

Use the day to recognise the people protecting parks and wildlife areas:

  • Share a ranger story, park update or conservation message from a trusted local or international source.
  • Thank or support ranger teams, park staff or conservation organisations where you have a real connection.
  • Use visits to parks and reserves responsibly: stay on marked routes, respect closures and do not disturb wildlife.
  • If there is a local event, use it to learn about ranger safety, protected areas and conservation work.
