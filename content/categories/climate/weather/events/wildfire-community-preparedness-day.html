About the event

Wildfire Community Preparedness Day is an annual campaign that helps neighbourhoods reduce the risk that a wildfire will reach homes and shared spaces. The work is practical: clear combustible material close to buildings, plan evacuation routes, support local fire services and learn how the home ignition zone works. It is not a day for spectacle or a single national ceremony. Its value comes from communities doing small, documented tasks before the hottest and driest part of the fire season. The campaign is coordinated in North America, but the preparation principles are useful wherever homes meet flammable vegetation. The 2026 day was Saturday 2 May; NFPA has not yet published the 2027 date, checked 27 November 2026.
