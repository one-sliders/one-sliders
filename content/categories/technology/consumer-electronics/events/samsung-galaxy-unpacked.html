About this event

Samsung's Unpacked events are the company's dedicated launch presentations for its Galaxy smartphone and foldable device lines, run independently of the wider CES and MWC trade-show calendar so Samsung controls its own announcement timing. Recent editions have leaned heavily on foldable-display hardware, a category Samsung has pushed harder than most competitors.
