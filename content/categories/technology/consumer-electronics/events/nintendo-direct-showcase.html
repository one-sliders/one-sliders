About this showcase

Nintendo Direct is Nintendo's own pre-recorded video presentation format for announcing games and hardware, run in place of a traditional press-conference keynote at trade shows. The format lets Nintendo control pacing and reveal timing precisely, and editions are watched live by millions on YouTube and Nintendo's own channels.
