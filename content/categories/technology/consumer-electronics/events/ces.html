About CES

CES in Las Vegas is the world's most influential consumer-technology show, where the industry unveils the products and ideas that will shape the year ahead. Run by the Consumer Technology Association each January, it fills the city with everything from TVs and cars to robotics and AI.

Next editionCES

The next CES is held in January across the Las Vegas Convention Center and surrounding venues. Thousands of exhibitors, from global giants to startups, use it to launch products and set the tech agenda for the year.
