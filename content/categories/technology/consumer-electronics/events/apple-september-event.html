About this showcase

Apple’s September event is the company’s most-watched annual product showcase because it is where the new iPhone generation usually becomes official. The local source has the latest verified edition as 9 September 2026 from Cupertino and online, so this page treats it as a completed launch archive until Apple announces the next event. It belongs in an online-showcase template rather than a travel template because most people follow it through Apple’s event stream and post-event product pages.
