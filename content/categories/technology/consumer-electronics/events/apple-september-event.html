About the event

Apple's September keynote is the company's biggest launch of the year — the stage where each new iPhone generation is unveiled, usually alongside new Apple Watch models. It streams from the Steve Jobs Theater at Apple Park in Cupertino to an audience of millions, and its announcements set the tone for the whole consumer-electronics season.
