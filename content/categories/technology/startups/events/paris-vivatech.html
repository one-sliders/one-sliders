About the event

Four days at Porte de Versailles with 200,000 attendees from 165 countries: VivaTech 2027 runs from 16 to 19 June 2027 in Paris. The organisers count 4,500 exhibitors and more than 15,000 startups across the halls, which makes it the largest technology and startup event in Europe. The last day is traditionally the one opened to the general public rather than to badge holders from the industry alone.
