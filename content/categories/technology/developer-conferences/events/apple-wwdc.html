About the event

Apple's Worldwide Developers Conference (WWDC) is the company's flagship event for software developers, held each June. It is where Apple reveals the next versions of iOS, macOS and its other platforms, setting the direction for its entire ecosystem for the year ahead.

There is no public ticket. The in-person day at Apple Park is free but capacity-limited, and places are drawn at random from applications by Apple Developer Program and Enterprise Program members, Entrepreneur Camp alumni and recent Swift Student Challenge winners. Apple does not cover travel or lodging, and the sessions stream free worldwide.
