About the event

Aufguss WM is the world championship for performance Aufguss, where sauna masters compete through heat control, towel technique, fragrance, music and storytelling. It turns the sauna session into a judged performance, with atmosphere and precision mattering as much as heat.

Travel tips
  • Pack for heat and cool-down time Bring swimwear or sauna clothing where required, sandals, a towel, water and a dry layer for after the final session.
  • Compare arrival times against sauna-session times; late arrivals are awkward when sessions, competitions or guided rounds run on fixed slots.
