About World Sauna Day

World Sauna Day celebrates the sauna and its deep roots in Finnish and Nordic culture, where it is a cherished ritual of health, relaxation and community. It invites people around the world to experience the sauna and the wellbeing traditions surrounding it.

Next editionWorld Sauna Day

The next World Sauna Day is marked on 25 April, with saunas, spas and enthusiasts hosting open sessions and events. It spotlights the cultural heritage of sauna bathing, recognised by UNESCO in Finland.

How to take part

Keep it simple and practical:

  • Plan a sauna session for the day.
  • Invite friends, family or your local sauna community.
  • Share a sauna story, photo or tradition.
  • Join a public community sauna event if one is available nearby.
  • Make the session about heat, recovery, cold dips if you use them, and respectful sauna culture.
