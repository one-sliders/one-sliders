About the event

Sauna & Wellness Expo Japan is a trade-focused sauna and wellness event for equipment, operators and hospitality ideas. Expect the emphasis to sit on sauna rooms, cold-plunge products, operational know-how and the business side of bathing culture.

Travel tips
  • Pack for heat and cool-down time Bring swimwear or sauna clothing where required, sandals, a towel, water and a dry layer for after the final session.
  • Compare arrival times against sauna-session times; late arrivals are awkward when sessions, competitions or guided rounds run on fixed slots.
