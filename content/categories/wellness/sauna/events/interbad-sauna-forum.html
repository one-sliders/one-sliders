About the event

interbad is the trade fair and congress for pools, saunas, spas and wellness, held every second year at Messe Stuttgart - next on 10 to 12 October 2028. The 2026 edition drew 7,500 trade visitors and around 300 exhibitors, with the DGfdB congress running alongside the hall and Sauna from Finland bringing a national pavilion of its roughly 200 member companies. It is a professional event: operators, builders and suppliers rather than sauna guests.

interbad Sauna Forum sits inside a broader pool, spa and wellness trade-fair context. Its sauna angle is practical and industry-facing, with attention on operators, equipment, facility ideas and the business of spa and bathing spaces.
