About Global Wellness Day

Global Wellness Day is a non-commercial, international day dedicated to living well, founded in 2012 and now marked in more than 150 countries. Built around the motto 'one day can change your whole life', it promotes simple, healthy habits through free events at spas, hotels and communities.

Next editionGlobal Wellness Day

The next Global Wellness Day falls on the second Saturday of June, with free classes, walks and wellbeing events hosted worldwide. Its manifesto of small daily changes aims to spark lasting healthy habits.

How to take part

Use the day for one healthy action you can actually repeat:

  • Choose a simple wellness activity: movement, rest, time outdoors, better sleep, hydration or social connection.
  • Invite someone else to join without making it competitive.
  • Use local wellness events if they are available, but keep the focus on wellbeing rather than shopping.
  • Pick one habit to carry into the next week.
