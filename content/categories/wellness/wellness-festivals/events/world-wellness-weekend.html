About World Wellness Weekend

World Wellness Weekend is a global celebration of health and wellbeing, inviting venues and communities in more than 150 countries to offer free activities - from fitness and yoga to nutrition and mindfulness. It aims to make wellbeing accessible and inspire year-round healthy habits.

Next editionWorld Wellness Weekend

The next World Wellness Weekend is held in late September, with thousands of free sessions worldwide across sport, nutrition, sleep, mindfulness and more. Gyms, spas, hotels and clubs open their doors to newcomers.

How to take part

Take part through a local or personal wellness activity:

  • Look for a community class, open session, outdoor activity or wellbeing event near you.
  • Choose something realistic: movement, recovery, mindfulness, food, sleep or social connection.
  • Invite someone who would benefit from a low-pressure activity.
  • If organising an activity, make the time, location, cost and accessibility clear.
