About this day

Global Running Day brings people around the world together through running and movement. The official campaign invites people to get moving in their own way, whether that means a short solo run, a group outing or the official virtual 5K. The next edition falls on 2 June 2027, keeping the day as a shared moment for clubs, schools, workplaces and individuals to celebrate the simple act of running.

How to take part

Take part by making running accessible and social:

  • Go for a short run, jog or walk-run at a level that fits your body.
  • Invite someone who might not normally join a running activity.
  • Use a safe route, visible clothing and realistic pacing.
  • Share the run or join a local group event if one is available.
