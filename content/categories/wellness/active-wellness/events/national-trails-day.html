About this day

National Trails Day is led by the American Hiking Society on the first Saturday in June. The next edition falls on 5 June 2027 and focuses on trail stewardship: finding or hosting local events, taking the National Trails Day pledge, cleaning up paths and supporting public lands. It is not one venue or one city; the useful way to read it is as a nationwide outdoor action day built around local trails.

How to take part

Use the day to get onto a trail and care for it:

  • Walk, hike, run or ride a trail that matches your ability and local conditions.
  • Check route rules before you go, including access, dogs, bikes, fire restrictions and weather.
  • Carry out what you carry in and leave the trail better than you found it.
  • Join a trail maintenance, cleanup or guided outing if one is available.
