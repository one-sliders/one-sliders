About the event

National Trails Day is led by the American Hiking Society and held each year in early June. The 34th edition took place on Saturday, 6 June 2026, focused on trail stewardship: finding or hosting local events, taking the National Trails Day pledge, cleaning up paths and supporting public lands. The American Hiking Society had not announced the 2027 date as of 25 November 2026. It is not one venue or one city; the useful way to read it is as a nationwide outdoor action day built around local trails.
