About the event

The World Junior Ice Hockey Championship is the top under-20 tournament in the sport and a huge event in Canada, played over the New Year holidays. It is a showcase for future NHL stars and a fierce contest between hockey's leading nations, with Canada the most successful country by a distance. The 2027 tournament is in Edmonton, 26 December - 5 January.
