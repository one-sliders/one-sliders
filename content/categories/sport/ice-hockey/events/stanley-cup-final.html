About Stanley Cup Final

The Stanley Cup Final is the championship series of the National Hockey League and the oldest trophy competition in North American professional sport, with the Cup first awarded in 1893. Each June the Eastern and Western Conference champions meet in a best-of-seven series played at the two finalists' home arenas across the United States and Canada. The Montreal Canadiens are the most decorated club with 24 Cups — a total no other franchise comes close to — and every player on the winning roster gets their name engraved on the trophy.

Next editionStanley Cup Final

The 2027 Stanley Cup Final is expected in June 2027, a best-of-seven series between the champions of the Eastern and Western Conferences. The teams — and therefore the host cities — are known only after four rounds of playoffs, and home-ice advantage goes to the club with the better regular-season record.
