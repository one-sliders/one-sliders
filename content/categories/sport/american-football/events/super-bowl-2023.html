About the game

Super Bowl LVII was played on 12 February 2023 at State Farm Stadium in Glendale, Arizona. The Kansas City Chiefs trailed the Philadelphia Eagles 24–14 at halftime and came back to win 38–35, settled by a late field goal after a second half in which the Chiefs scored on every possession. Patrick Mahomes, playing on an injured ankle, was named MVP. Rihanna performed the halftime show and used it to reveal her pregnancy; the show reached 118.7 million viewers.
