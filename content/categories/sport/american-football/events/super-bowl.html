About Super Bowl

The Super Bowl is the championship game of the National Football League and the most-watched television broadcast in the United States every year, routinely drawing more than 120 million viewers. Played on a Sunday in February at a neutral stadium chosen years in advance, it settles the NFL title in a single winner-take-all game between the AFC and NFC champions, who lift the Vince Lombardi Trophy. The halftime show and the commercials have become cultural events in their own right. The New England Patriots and Pittsburgh Steelers share the record with six titles each, while the Kansas City Chiefs have driven the modern era.

Next editionSuper Bowl

The next Super Bowl is played on 14 February 2027 at SoFi Stadium in Inglewood, California. It is a single game between the champions of the AFC and NFC; there are no group stages or replays, and the winner is decided that night, overtime if needed.
