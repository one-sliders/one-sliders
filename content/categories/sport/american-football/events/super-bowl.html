About the game

The Super Bowl is the championship game of the National Football League, played on a Sunday in February at a neutral stadium the league names years in advance, between the AFC and NFC champions decided two weeks earlier. It is the most-watched television broadcast in the United States every year, and the halftime show and commercials have become events in their own right. The New England Patriots and Pittsburgh Steelers share the record with six titles each. Each edition has its own page with that year's teams, score, MVP and halftime show; this page always points to the next one.
