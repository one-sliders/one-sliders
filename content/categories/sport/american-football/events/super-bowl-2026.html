About the game

Super Bowl LX was played on 8 February 2026 at Levi's Stadium in Santa Clara. The Seattle Seahawks beat the New England Patriots 29–13 and held them scoreless through three quarters, sacking Drake Maye six times and forcing three turnovers. Jason Myers kicked five field goals, a Super Bowl record, and running back Kenneth Walker III was named MVP after 135 yards on 27 carries. Bad Bunny headlined the halftime show, joined by Lady Gaga and Ricky Martin.
