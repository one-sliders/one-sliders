About the game

Super Bowl LXIII is scheduled for Sunday 11 February 2029 at Allegiant Stadium in Las Vegas, home of the Raiders — the stadium's second Super Bowl after LVIII in 2024. The NFL confirmed the host on 30 March 2026. The two teams are set by the AFC and NFC Championship Games in late January 2029, and the halftime performer is announced during the preceding season.
