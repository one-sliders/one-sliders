About the game

Super Bowl LIX was played on 9 February 2025 at the Caesars Superdome in New Orleans, a rematch of the 2023 final. The Philadelphia Eagles routed the Kansas City Chiefs 40–22 and denied them the first three-peat in Super Bowl history. Jalen Hurts was named MVP after two passing touchdowns, one rushing touchdown and a Super Bowl record 72 rushing yards by a quarterback. Kendrick Lamar headlined the halftime show.
