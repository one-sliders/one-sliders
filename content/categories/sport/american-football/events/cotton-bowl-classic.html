About the event

The Cotton Bowl Classic, first played in 1937, is one of American college football's oldest bowl games and part of the sport's historic 'New Year's Six' bowl tradition. It now serves as a College Football Playoff quarterfinal in the playoff's rotation, played at AT&T Stadium, home of the Dallas Cowboys.
