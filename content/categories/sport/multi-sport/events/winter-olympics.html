About the event

The Winter Olympic Games bring together the world's best skiers, skaters, sliders and snowboarders every four years - a fortnight of speed and spectacle in the mountains. First held in Chamonix in 1924, the Games have grown from a handful of events to a global showcase of winter sport. The 2030 Games are hosted across the French Alps.

Ticket availability and entry conditions should be confirmed through the organizer before purchase.

Tickets

Tickets are sold by the Games' own organising committee on a single official platform at the same prices worldwide, and demand is high enough that buyers apply to a lottery rather than buy on sight. Hospitality that includes a ticket comes only through the IOC's appointed provider — anything bought outside those channels can be cancelled at the gate.
