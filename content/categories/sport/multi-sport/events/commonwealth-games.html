About the event

The Commonwealth Games bring together athletes from the nations and territories of the Commonwealth every four years, in a friendly, inclusive event nicknamed the 'Friendly Games'. Uniquely, it fully integrates para-sport into the main programme. Glasgow hosts a streamlined 2027 edition.
