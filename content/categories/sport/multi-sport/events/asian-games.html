About the event

The Asian Games are the second-largest multi-sport event in the world after the Olympics, bringing together thousands of athletes from across Asia every four years. The programme mixes Olympic sports with regional ones such as kabaddi, sepaktakraw and wushu. Nagoya, Japan hosts in 2026.

Ticket availability and entry conditions should be confirmed through the organizer before purchase.
