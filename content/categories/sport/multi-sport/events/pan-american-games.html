About the event

Lima, Peru hosts the Pan American Games from 23 July to 8 August 2027. Panam Sports moved the window a week later in January 2026 because the 2027 international calendar was too crowded - the original dates were 16 July to 1 August, and the shift also reduces the overlap with the Women's World Cup in Brazil. Panam Sports now publishes the 23 July - 8 August window. Individual competition venues across Lima have not yet been announced on the organiser's site.
