About the event

Monaco gets two Formula E races in 2027, on Saturday 1 and Sunday 2 May - rounds 8 and 9 of a 21-round season. A double-header changes the trip: one weekend covers two race days on the same street circuit, which is not how most rounds of the championship are scheduled.

These are rounds 8 and 9 of the FIA Formula E World Championship, Season 13, which runs to 21 rounds in total. Monaco sits in the first half of the calendar, and it is one of the few venues on it that holds two rounds in the same weekend.

Not published as of 2026-10-19. The championship calendar confirms the two Monaco rounds on 1 and 2 May 2027 but carries no grandstand map, no spectator zones and no prices, so which vantage point is worth the money cannot be stated for this edition yet.
