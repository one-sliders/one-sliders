About this event

Goodwood Festival of Speed is built around a hillclimb course through the grounds of Goodwood House rather than a closed circuit, letting the event showcase everything from historic Formula 1 cars to current manufacturer prototypes and concept cars in a single programme. It has grown into one of the most significant dates on the classic and current motorsport calendar.
