About the event

Roland-Garros 2027 runs from 17 May to 6 June 2027 at the Stade Roland-Garros. That is a three-week span, longer than the fortnight most people picture, so the date you travel on decides whether you see early rounds or a final. The tournament has not published its 2027 ticket sale dates: the only ticketing notice on its site so far is a warning about ticket scams, which is worth reading before buying anywhere else.

Court Simonne-Mathieu seats 5,000, against the main Court Philippe-Chatrier, whose capacity Roland-Garros does not publish in the same place. In the first week the smaller courts carry full rounds, so a cheaper seat sees more tennis per day; from the quarter-finals on, play concentrates on Chatrier and the cheap seat sees nothing.
