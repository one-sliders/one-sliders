About the event

The US Open runs 29 August to 12 September 2027 at the USTA Billie Jean King National Tennis Center in Flushing Meadows - fifteen days of main draw since the tournament added the opening Sunday, on hard courts, under the roofs of Arthur Ashe and Louis Armstrong. Qualifying and Fan Week fill the week before. Ticketing-site listings for 2027 disagree by a day in both directions; the dates here are the ones on the WTA's own tournament page.

Tickets

Tickets are sold through the tournament's own ticket pages and its official ticketing partner. Buyers choose between reserved seats in Arthur Ashe Stadium, Louis Armstrong Stadium or the Grandstand, and a Grounds Pass that gives first-come, first-served access to the remaining courts.
