About the event

Centre Court seats 14,979; a grounds pass gets you onto 17 outside courts and Henman Hill. First played 1877.

2027 is the 140th Championships, with Jannik Sinner going for a third straight title after beating Alcaraz in 2025 and Zverev in 2026, and Linda Noskova defending her first major. Finals start at 16:00 since 2025, so the whole second Saturday and Sunday build towards them. Line judges are gone: electronic calling on every court since 2025. Prize money hit a record 64.2 million pounds in 2026.

No.1 Court is the value seat: 12,345 places under a roof, top-ten players most days in week one, at a fraction of Centre Court's price. In the first four days a grounds pass is the best buy of all: 64 singles matches a day across 17 outside courts, two metres from the players, and Henman Hill for the show courts on the big screen. Centre Court is for the second week, when the draw has narrowed to the names you came for.
