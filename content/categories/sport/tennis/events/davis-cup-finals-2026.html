About the event

The eight-team finals run at Bologna Fiere from 24 to 29 November 2026. The field was completed in the September qualifiers: Italy are in as holders and hosts, joined by Germany, Spain, Great Britain, Austria, Canada, Czechia and South Korea, who reached the quarter-finals for the first time. The quarter-finals are Czechia v Canada and Italy v South Korea on 24 and 25 November and Great Britain v Germany and Austria v Spain on 26 November, with semi-finals on 27 and 28 November and the final at 15:00 CET on Sunday 29 November. Tickets are on sale through the official Davis Cup ticketing site.

The Davis Cup Finals decide the men's world team championship in tennis. The competition began in 1900 as a challenge between the United States and the British Isles, using a trophy bought by Dwight Davis, and grew into the largest annual international team competition in sport measured by nations entered. For most of its history it ran as home-and-away ties spread across the year, played over three days on a surface chosen by the host — a format that produced some of the sport's most partisan atmospheres. Since 2019 the closing stage has been condensed into a single finals week at one venue, where qualified nations meet in ties of two singles rubbers and a doubles rubber. The change traded home advantage for a compact event but kept the competition's defining feature: players representing a country rather than themselves. The United States hold the record with thirty-two titles. The 2026 finals week runs from 24 to 29 November in Spain.
