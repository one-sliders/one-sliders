About this event

The WTA Finals close the women's tennis season, bringing together the eight highest-ranked singles players and the eight leading doubles teams from the year's results. It is the hardest field to qualify for in the sport, since entry is earned by ranking points accumulated over the whole season rather than by a draw. The format opens with two round-robin groups, which guarantees every qualifier at least three matches and means a player can lose and still advance, before semi-finals and a final decide the title. The event dates to 1972 and Martina Navratilova holds the record with eight singles titles. It has moved host cities repeatedly over its history, from New York and Los Angeles through Madrid, Istanbul, Singapore and Shenzhen, and is currently staged in Riyadh under a multi-year agreement with the Saudi federation. The 2026 edition is scheduled from 1 to 8 November in Saudi Arabia.

Hotels

Recommended areasOlaya; Diplomatic Quarter; King Abdullah Financial District

Olaya is Riyadh’s main hotel and business district and the most convenient base, sitting on the metro line that runs the length of the city. The Diplomatic Quarter is quieter and greener; the financial district is newer and more corporate.

Tips

TransportUse the Riyadh Metro or ride-hailing

The metro runs the length of Olaya Street and connects the main hotel district to the rest of the city. Ride-hailing is widely used and usually the quickest option for cross-city trips outside metro hours.
