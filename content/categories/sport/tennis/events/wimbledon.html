About this event

Wimbledon is the grass-court Grand Slam tournament held at the All England Club in London. The 2027 Championships run from 28 June to 11 July, with future tournament dates already published through 2030. Visitor planning should follow the official ticketing, Queue and transport guidance because demand and day schedules can shift.
