About the event

The Qatar Grand Prix at the Lusail International Circuit near Doha is MotoGP's landmark night race, run under floodlights across the desert. It has frequently opened the season, and its long main straight produces slipstreaming battles and photo-finishes. The 2027 race is on 11-13 April.

Tickets

Grandstand and general-admission tickets for each Grand Prix are sold through the championship's own ticket shop, with each circuit's allocation released when the season's sale opens. Paddock-side VIP Village and Premier packages are the official hospitality route and are sold separately.
