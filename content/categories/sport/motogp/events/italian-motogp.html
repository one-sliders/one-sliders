About the event

The Italian Grand Prix is held at Mugello, a fast, undulating circuit in the Tuscan hills north of Florence, framed by a huge and colourful home crowd. Its kilometre-long main straight and flowing corners make it a rider favourite, and Valentino Rossi's seven straight wins here are the stuff of legend. Mugello is round eight, run 28-30 May 2027.

The 2027 Italian Grand Prix runs 28-30 May at Mugello in Tuscany. The long main straight produces some of the highest top speeds of the year, and the hillside crowd makes it one of the season's great atmospheres.

Tickets

Grandstand and general-admission tickets for each Grand Prix are sold through the championship's own ticket shop, with each circuit's allocation released when the season's sale opens. Paddock-side VIP Village and Premier packages are the official hospitality route and are sold separately.
