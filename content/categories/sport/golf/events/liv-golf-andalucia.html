About LIV Golf Andalucía

LIV Golf Andalucia is staged at Real Club Valderrama near Sotogrande, the fearsome Robert Trent Jones design that hosted the 1997 Ryder Cup. Tight, tree-lined and famous for its treacherous par-5 17th, it is one of the toughest courses the LIV series visits.

Next editionLIV Golf Andalucía

The next LIV Golf Andalucia is contested over 54 holes at Valderrama, with the individual and team titles decided across three days. The demanding cork-oak-lined layout tends to produce higher scoring than most LIV stops.
