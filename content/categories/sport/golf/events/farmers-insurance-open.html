About Farmers Insurance Open

The Farmers Insurance Open is played on the clifftop Torrey Pines Golf Course above the Pacific in San Diego - a municipal layout tough enough to host the US Open. The South Course's length and ocean wind make it one of the sternest regular-season tests on the PGA Tour.

Next editionFarmers Insurance Open

The next Farmers Insurance Open is contested across Torrey Pines' South and North courses in San Diego, with the closing rounds on the longer South. Tiger Woods has a storied history on these cliffs.
