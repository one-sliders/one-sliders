About The Sentry

The Sentry opens the PGA Tour season each January, gathering the previous season's tournament winners and leading FedExCup players for a no-cut, big-purse start to the year. After nearly three decades at Kapalua in Hawaii, the event moves for 2027 to the famous clifftop Torrey Pines Golf Course in San Diego, with Sentry Insurance as the new title sponsor.

Next editionThe Sentry

The 2027 Sentry is played 27-30 January at Torrey Pines Golf Course in San Diego - a new home for the season-opening winners' event. The Pacific-clifftop South Course, a past US Open venue, offers a sterner test than the wide-open Kapalua Plantation it replaces.
