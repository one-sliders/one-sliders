About AIG Women's Open

The AIG Women's Open is one of the five majors in women's golf and the game's most prestigious links championship, played on the same historic British and Irish courses as the men's Open. Wind and weather make it a demanding, unpredictable test at the height of summer.

Next editionAIG Women's Open

The next AIG Women's Open is played on a championship links course, this edition at Royal St George's in Kent. As a major it draws the strongest field in women's golf to battle the elements for one of the sport's biggest titles.
