About the event

LIV Golf, launched in 2022 with Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund as its financial backer, uses a team format and 54-hole no-cut structure that breaks sharply from traditional PGA Tour scheduling. Its arrival triggered a prolonged split in professional golf, with top players signing across both tours and litigation between LIV and the PGA Tour running for years afterward.
