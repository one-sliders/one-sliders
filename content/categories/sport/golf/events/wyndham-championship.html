About this event

The Wyndham Championship is traditionally the final regular-season event of the PGA Tour calendar before the FedExCup Playoffs begin, making it the last chance for players near the playoff cutline to qualify. Sedgefield Country Club, a Donald Ross design, has hosted the event for decades.
