About LIV Golf UK

LIV Golf United Kingdom is played at the JCB Golf & Country Club in Rocester, Staffordshire - the private course built by the construction-equipment giant of the same name. Its manicured, water-laden layout is one of Britain's newest championship venues.

Next editionLIV Golf UK

The next LIV Golf United Kingdom is decided over 54 holes at the JCB course, individual and team competitions combined. The immaculate, lake-dotted design is a showcase venue built specifically for elite events.
