About Butterfield Bermuda Championship

The Butterfield Bermuda Championship is a PGA Tour event at the government-owned Port Royal Golf Course, perched on the Atlantic cliffs of Bermuda. Ocean wind is the course's main defence, and the spectacular par-3 16th plays right along the sea.

Next editionButterfield Bermuda Championship
