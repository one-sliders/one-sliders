About Biltmore Championship Asheville

The Biltmore Championship is a new PGA Tour FedExCup Fall event at The Cliffs at Walnut Cove near Asheville, North Carolina - the Tour's first visit to the Blue Ridge Mountains in more than 80 years. The cool, elevated setting offers a scenic mountain test to open the fall season.
