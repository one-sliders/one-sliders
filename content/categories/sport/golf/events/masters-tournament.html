About Masters Tournament

The Masters Tournament is golf's most iconic major, played every April at Augusta National Golf Club in Georgia - the only major held at the same course each year. Its azaleas, green jacket and Amen Corner are woven into the sport's identity, and an invitation is one of the hardest tickets in golf. Jack Nicklaus holds the record with six green jackets; Tiger Woods has five. The 2027 tournament is on 8-11 April.

Next editionMasters Tournament

The 2027 Masters runs 8-11 April at Augusta National. The field is small and invitation-only, past champions are exempt for life, and the Sunday leader plays for the green jacket presented by the previous year's winner.
