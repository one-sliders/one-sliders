About the event

The Valspar Championship is played on the Copperhead Course at Innisbrook near Tampa, one of Florida's toughest layouts. Its brutal three-hole closing stretch - the 'Snake Pit' - regularly decides the tournament and separates it from the state's easier resort courses.
