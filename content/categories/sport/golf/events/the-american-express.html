About The American Express

The American Express is a sun-soaked pro-am in the California desert around La Quinta and PGA West, long a celebrity-studded fixture of the Palm Springs area. Multiple easy-scoring courses make it one of the lowest-scoring weeks on the PGA Tour.

Next editionThe American Express

The next American Express rotates the opening rounds across desert courses at PGA West and La Quinta before the final round on a single layout. Birdies come in bunches on the resort-style setups.
