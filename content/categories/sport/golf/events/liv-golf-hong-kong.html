About LIV Golf Hong Kong

LIV Golf Hong Kong is played at the historic Hong Kong Golf Club at Fanling, a colonial-era course tucked into the New Territories. Its tight, tree-lined fairways and small greens make it a strategic test in the heart of one of Asia's great cities.
