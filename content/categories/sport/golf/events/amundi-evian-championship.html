About The Amundi Evian Championship

The Amundi Evian Championship is a women's golf major played at the picturesque Evian Resort on the shores of Lake Geneva in the French Alps. Elevated to major status in 2013, it is the only women's major held in continental Europe and one of the most scenic events in the sport.

Next editionThe Amundi Evian Championship

The next Evian Championship is played at the Evian Resort Golf Club in Evian-les-Bains, France, overlooking Lake Geneva. As a major it draws the world's best women professionals to one of golf's most beautiful settings.
