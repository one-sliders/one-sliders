About HSBC Women's World Championship

The HSBC Women's World Championship is one of the LPGA Tour's marquee events in Asia, played at Sentosa Golf Club in Singapore. Billed as 'Asia's Major', it draws one of the strongest fields outside the majors to a tropical, resort-island setting. The 2027 tournament is on 26 February - 1 March.

Next editionHSBC Women's World Championship
