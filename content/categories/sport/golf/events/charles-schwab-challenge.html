About Charles Schwab Challenge

The Charles Schwab Challenge is played at Colonial Country Club in Fort Worth - 'Hogan's Alley', named for Ben Hogan's five wins there. A short, tight, tree-lined classic, it rewards precision, and the champion receives a plaid jacket in a nod to the club's heritage.

Next editionCharles Schwab Challenge
