About the event

The ISPS Handa Women's Scottish Open is a links championship co-sanctioned by the LPGA and Ladies European Tour, played the week before the AIG Women's Open. Wind and firm turf at Dundonald Links near Troon make it ideal preparation for the year's oldest women's major.
