About the tournament

The 2027 Ryder Cup is played at Adare Manor in County Limerick from 13 to 19 September 2027, the first staged in Ireland since 2006 and the hundredth anniversary of the match. Luke Donald captains Europe for a third time and Jim Furyk leads the United States. General admission is already gone: the public ballot ran from 3 to 29 June 2026 and the organiser announced on 31 July 2026 that it had sold out, more than twelve times oversubscribed. Ballot prices were EUR 499 a day for the three match days, EUR 179 for the Thursday including the opening ceremony and from EUR 89 for practice days.
