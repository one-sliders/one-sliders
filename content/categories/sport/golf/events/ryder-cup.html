About Ryder Cup

The Ryder Cup is golf's fiercest team event - a biennial match-play contest between the United States and Europe that inspires a passion and pressure unlike anything else in the sport. There is no prize money; players compete purely for their team and continent over three days of foursomes, fourballs and singles. The 2027 edition is at Adare Manor in Ireland on 13-19 September.

Next editionRyder Cup

The 2027 Ryder Cup is held 13-19 September at Adare Manor in County Limerick - the first time the Republic of Ireland hosts the match. Twelve US players face twelve Europeans in a format that alternates continents every two years.
