About U.S. Women's Open pres. by Ally

The US Women's Open is the most prestigious championship in women's golf and, like the men's version, the toughest test - run by the USGA on demanding courses with the largest purse in the women's game. Winning it is the defining achievement of a female golfer's career.

Next editionU.S. Women's Open pres. by Ally

The next US Women's Open is played at a demanding USGA-selected course, this edition at Inverness Club in Toledo, Ohio. Thick rough and firm greens make it the sternest examination in women's golf, for the sport's biggest prize.
