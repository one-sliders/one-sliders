About Sony Open in Hawaii

The Sony Open in Hawaii is the PGA Tour's first full-field event of the year, played at the tight, tree-lined Waialae Country Club in Honolulu right after the season-opening Sentry. Trade winds and small greens on the classic Seth Raynor design make accuracy essential.
