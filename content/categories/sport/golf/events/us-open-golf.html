About the event

The US Open is one of golf's four majors, run by the USGA and famous for being the toughest test in the sport - narrow fairways, thick rough and lightning-fast greens that punish any mistake. Open to any professional or amateur who qualifies, it rotates around demanding courses across the United States. The 2027 edition is at Pebble Beach.
