About the event

The Good Good Championship is a new PGA Tour event at Omni Barton Creek's Fazio Canyons course in Austin, Texas, backed by the popular Good Good golf-media brand. It marks the Tour's return to Austin and aims to draw a younger, digital-savvy audience to the fall schedule.
