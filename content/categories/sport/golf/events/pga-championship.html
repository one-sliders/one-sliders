About PGA Championship

The PGA Championship is one of golf's four majors, run by the PGA of America and now played in May. Historically a match-play event and long the final major of the year, it is known for strong fields and demanding setups. Walter Hagen and Jack Nicklaus share the record with five titles each. The 2027 championship is on 20-23 May at PGA Frisco in Texas.

Next editionPGA Championship

The 2027 PGA Championship runs 20-23 May at the Fields Ranch East course at PGA Frisco, Texas. Unlike the Masters it moves to a new course most years, and its field is among the strongest of any major.
