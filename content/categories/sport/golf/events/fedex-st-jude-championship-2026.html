About the event

The FedEx St. Jude Championship is the first event of the PGA Tour's FedExCup Playoffs, played at TPC Southwind in Memphis. Only the season's top players qualify, and it begins the three-event run that decides the FedExCup and its huge bonus, while supporting St. Jude Children's Research Hospital.
