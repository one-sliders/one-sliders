About John Deere Classic

The John Deere Classic is a birdie-friendly PGA Tour stop at TPC Deere Run in Silvis, Illinois, played the week before The Open Championship. It has a reputation for giving young and first-time winners their breakthrough, and for flying its champion to the UK for the major.

Next editionJohn Deere Classic

The next John Deere Classic is played at TPC Deere Run in the Quad Cities. Low, aggressive scoring is the norm on the receptive layout, with a charter jet on offer to reach The Open in time.
