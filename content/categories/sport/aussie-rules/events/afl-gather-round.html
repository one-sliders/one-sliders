About AFL Gather Round

Gather Round is the AFL's 'festival of footy' - a round in which every match is played in a single state, South Australia, over one weekend, turning the host state into the centre of the football world. Introduced in 2023, it has quickly become a fan favourite.

Next editionAFL Gather Round

The next Gather Round stages a full round of AFL matches across venues in South Australia, from Adelaide Oval to regional grounds. Fans travel in from around the country for a concentrated weekend of football.
