About Dreamtime at the G

Dreamtime at the 'G is the AFL's flagship Indigenous Round fixture, an Essendon-Richmond clash at the MCG that celebrates the contribution of Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander players to the game. It features a pre-match Welcome to Country and the Long Walk tradition.

Next editionDreamtime at the G

The next Dreamtime at the 'G is played at the MCG as the centrepiece of the AFL's Sir Doug Nicholls Indigenous Round. Essendon and Richmond meet in a fixture that blends football with a celebration of Indigenous culture.
