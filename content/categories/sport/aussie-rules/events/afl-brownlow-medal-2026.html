About the event

The Brownlow Medal is Australian rules football's most prestigious individual honour, awarded to the AFL's best and fairest player of the home-and-away season. Decided by umpires' votes and revealed at a glamorous count on the eve of the Grand Final, 'Brownlow night' is a major event in its own right.
