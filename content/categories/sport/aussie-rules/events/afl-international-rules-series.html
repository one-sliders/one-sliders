About AFL International Rules Series

The International Rules Series is a hybrid contest between Australian rules footballers and Gaelic footballers from Ireland, played under a compromise set of rules with a round ball. A periodic representative series, it is the main international outlet for the otherwise domestic AFL.

Next editionAFL International Rules Series

The next International Rules Series pits an Australian representative side against Ireland under hybrid rules, alternating hosting between the two nations. It is a rare chance to see AFL stars in international colours.
