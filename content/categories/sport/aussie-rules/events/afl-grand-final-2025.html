About the event

The AFL Grand Final is the championship-deciding match of Australian rules football and one of the biggest days on the Australian sporting calendar. Held on the last Saturday of September at the Melbourne Cricket Ground before a 100,000 crowd, it crowns the season's premiers. Carlton, Collingwood and Essendon share the record with 16 premierships each.
