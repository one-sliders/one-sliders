About AFL Anzac Day

The Anzac Day clash between Essendon and Collingwood is one of the AFL's most emotional and best-attended fixtures, played each 25 April at the MCG to commemorate Australia's war dead. Since 1995 it has drawn huge crowds and opens with a solemn pre-match ceremony.

Next editionAFL Anzac Day

The next Anzac Day clash is played on 25 April at the MCG, with a moving commemorative service before the first bounce. Essendon and Collingwood contest the Anzac Medal, awarded to the best player afield.
