About this event

The Indian Premier League Final decides the champion of the world's richest domestic Twenty20 cricket league, whose broadcast and sponsorship value have made it one of the most commercially significant properties in global sport. IPL franchises draw a mix of Indian internationals and top overseas players auctioned into the league each season.
