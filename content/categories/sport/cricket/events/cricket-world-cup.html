About Cricket World Cup

The Cricket World Cup is the pinnacle of one-day international cricket, contested every four years since 1975 and followed by huge audiences across South Asia, Australia and England. Australia lead all nations with six titles. Southern Africa gets its turn as host for the 2027 edition, staged jointly by South Africa, Zimbabwe and Namibia between October and December.

Next editionCricket World Cup

The 2027 Cricket World Cup runs from October to December, co-hosted by South Africa, Zimbabwe and Namibia. Fourteen teams contest the 50-over tournament, with the host nations' grounds staging matches through to the final.
