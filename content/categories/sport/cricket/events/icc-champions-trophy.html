About the event

The ICC Champions Trophy is a one-day international tournament contested by the top-ranked cricket nations, historically positioned as a shorter, higher-stakes alternative to the 50-over World Cup. It has been played intermittently since 1998, with editions occasionally skipped as the international cricket calendar shifted toward T20 formats.
