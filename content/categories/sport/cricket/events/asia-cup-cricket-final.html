About the event

The Asia Cup is the premier international cricket tournament in Asia, contested by the continent's leading nations and alternating between one-day and T20 formats. India are by far the most successful side. The final decides the champion of Asia.

Coming years

2027

The Asian Cricket Council had not confirmed the 2027 tournament as of 2026-10-23, so there is no date for the final. What is reported: Bangladesh as host in the 50-over format per the ACC's own expression-of-interest document, a provisional window of 18 June to 4 July 2027, six teams in two groups of three feeding a Super 4, thirteen matches in all, and Mirpur, Chattogram and Sylhet as likely venues. All of it is provisional - the ACC publishes the schedule closer to the tournament.
