    About UCI Road World Championships

    The UCI Road World Championships crown the world champions in road cycling each autumn, with the winners earning the right to wear the rainbow jersey for a year. The elite men's and women's road races are the blue-riband events, run over a demanding one-day circuit, and the host city changes annually.

    Next editionUCI Road World Championships

    The UCI Road World Championships are held over a week each September, with elite men's and women's road races, time trials and a mixed relay. The host city changes every year, taking the sport to new audiences around the world.
