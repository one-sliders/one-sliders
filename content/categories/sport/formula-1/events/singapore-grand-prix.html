About Singapore Grand Prix

The Singapore Grand Prix made history in 2008 as Formula 1's first-ever night race, run under floodlights around the Marina Bay street circuit. Heat and humidity, close walls and one of the longest lap times of the year turn it into a physical endurance test for drivers as much as a race. Sebastian Vettel holds the record with five wins here. This edition runs 9-11 October 2026.

Next editionSingapore Grand Prix

The 2026 Singapore Grand Prix runs 9-11 October under the lights at Marina Bay, past the city's illuminated skyline. Safety cars are almost guaranteed on the tight, bumpy streets, and drivers can lose several kilos in a single race in the tropical heat.
