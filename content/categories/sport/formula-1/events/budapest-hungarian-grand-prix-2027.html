About the event

The 2027 Hungarian Grand Prix is round 13 of a 24-race season and runs from Friday 30 July to Sunday 1 August 2027 at the Hungaroring, north-east of Budapest at Mogyorod. The weekend is sold both as single days and as passes - Friday, Saturday, Sunday, a two-day and a three-day ticket - and all five are already listed on the resale market, which makes this one of the few races where the Friday is a product of its own.

Round 13 of a 24-race season, run over 70 laps of the 4.381-kilometre Hungaroring on 1 August 2027. The circuit is narrow and twisty, which is why the last ten years have thrown up winners from outside the front row of the championship - Esteban Ocon in 2021, Oscar Piastri in 2024 - rather than a procession. The weekend starts on Friday 30 July.

The weekend is sold as five separate products: single days for Friday, Saturday and Sunday, a two-day ticket covering Saturday and Sunday, and a three-day pass. All five were listed on the resale market in October 2026. That split is what makes the Friday worth considering on its own here, which is not true at every race.
