About the event

MADRING holds its second Spanish Grand Prix on 10 to 12 September 2027, at the circuit built around the IFEMA fairgrounds on Avenida del Partenon in the north-east of the city. The debut weekend in 2026 drew 352,618 people across the three days. Madrid's contract with Formula 1 runs for ten years, and 2027 is the first season in which Spain has only this one race.

MADRING holds its second Grand Prix on 10 to 12 September 2027, and 2027 is the first year Spain has only this race - Barcelona is off the calendar. The circuit runs 5.4 kilometres over 22 turns around the IFEMA fairgrounds, mixing public road sections such as the Subida de las Carcavas with purpose-built stretches. The debut weekend drew 352,618 people.

Prices for 2027 were not published as of 20 October 2026, so value cannot be compared yet. What the circuit does say: La Monumental at Curve 12 is the big one at 45,000 seats, Norte sits at Curve 18 by the North Convention Centre and is where hospitality guests are placed, and El Parque overlooks the final turn with the Juan Carlos I park behind it. Metro line 8 reaches IFEMA directly.
