About Canadian Grand Prix

The Canadian Grand Prix has been held on the Circuit Gilles-Villeneuve - a semi-permanent track on Ile Notre-Dame in the St Lawrence River - since 1978, and it consistently delivers drama. Long straights, heavy braking zones and the infamous 'Wall of Champions' at the final chicane make it a favourite of drivers and a reliable producer of surprises. Michael Schumacher and Lewis Hamilton share the record with seven wins each. The 2027 race is on 11-13 June.

Next editionCanadian Grand Prix

The 2027 Canadian Grand Prix runs 11-13 June in Montreal. The circuit's low-grip surface and long flat-out sections are hard on brakes and engines, and its position beside the river makes it one of the most scenic stops on the calendar.
