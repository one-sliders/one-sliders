About the event

The United States Grand Prix has moved around the country for decades but found a lasting home in 2012 at the Circuit of the Americas in Austin, Texas. COTA's steep uphill first corner, fast esses and modern layout have made it a fan favourite, and F1's American boom has turned the race weekend into a festival. Lewis Hamilton has won the US Grand Prix six times across two venues. The 2026 race is on 23-25 October.

Tickets

Each Grand Prix is staged by a local promoter licensed by Formula 1, and that promoter sells the grandstand and general-admission tickets for its own race — often through the circuit's own box office. Official hospitality is sold alongside them on Formula 1's ticketing platform.
