About Monaco Grand Prix

The Monaco Grand Prix is the most glamorous and demanding race in Formula 1 — a tight street circuit through Monte Carlo where overtaking is almost impossible and qualifying is everything. Run since 1929 and part of the world championship from its first season in 1950, its barriers, tunnel and harbour chicane punish the smallest mistake. Ayrton Senna's six wins here are the all-time record. The 2027 race is on 4-6 June.

Next editionMonaco Grand Prix

The 2027 Monaco Grand Prix runs 4-6 June around the streets of Monte Carlo. At about 3.3 km it is the shortest lap on the calendar and the race distance is cut to roughly 260 km - the only Grand Prix under the usual 305 km - because the cars are so slow through the corners.
