About the event

The Sao Paulo Grand Prix - long known as the Brazilian Grand Prix - is one of Formula 1's great racing venues, held at the short, anti-clockwise Interlagos circuit since 1973. Its bumpy surface, elevation changes and unpredictable weather produce chaos and championship deciders, and the passionate Brazilian crowd made heroes of Senna, Piquet and Massa. Alain Prost's six wins lead the all-time list. The 2026 race is on 6-8 November.

Tickets

Each Grand Prix is staged by a local promoter licensed by Formula 1, and that promoter sells the grandstand and general-admission tickets for its own race — often through the circuit's own box office. Official hospitality is sold alongside them on Formula 1's ticketing platform.
