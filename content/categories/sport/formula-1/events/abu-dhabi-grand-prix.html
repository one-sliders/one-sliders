About Abu Dhabi Grand Prix

The Abu Dhabi Grand Prix has closed the Formula 1 season since 2009, a day-to-night race under floodlights around the Yas Marina Circuit. Its marina, hotel and grandstands make it one of the sport's showcase venues, and as the finale it has repeatedly settled the world championship - most dramatically in 2021. Max Verstappen and Lewis Hamilton share the win record with five each. The 2026 race is on 4-6 December.

Next editionAbu Dhabi Grand Prix

The 2026 Abu Dhabi Grand Prix runs 4-6 December at Yas Marina, the traditional season finale. The twilight start means the race begins in daylight and finishes under lights, and a recent layout change added faster, flowing corners to improve overtaking.
