About the event

The College World Series is the climax of American college baseball - a double-elimination tournament held each June at Charles Schwab Field in Omaha, Nebraska, where eight teams battle for the national championship. It is a major event on the US sporting calendar and a showcase for future Major League talent. USC's twelve titles are the most of any program. The 2027 series is on 18-28 June.
