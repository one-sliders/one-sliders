About the event

The World Baseball Classic is the sport's premier international tournament, pitting national teams - stocked with Major League stars - against each other in a World Cup-style event. It has done more than anything to grow baseball's global profile. Japan are the most successful nation with three titles. The 2027 final stage is in Miami, 5-17 March.
