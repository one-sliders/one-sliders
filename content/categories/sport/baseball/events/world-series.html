About the event

The World Series is the championship of Major League Baseball — a best-of-seven showdown every October between the champions of the American League and National League, first contested in 1903. There is no fixed host: games alternate between the two finalists' home ballparks, so the venue is only known once the pennants are decided. The New York Yankees are by far the most successful franchise with 27 titles, more than double any rival, and remain the benchmark every October.
