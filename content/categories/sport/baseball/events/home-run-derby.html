About this event

The 2027 T-Mobile Home Run Derby is scheduled for Monday, 12 July 2027 at Wrigley Field in Chicago. The date and venue move the event from the completed 2026 Philadelphia edition to MLB All-Star Week at one of baseball's oldest ballparks.
