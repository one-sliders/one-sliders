About the event

The 31st Ljubljana Marathon is a two-day weekend, 16 and 17 October 2027, with four races: the marathon over 42.195 kilometres, the Zavarovalnica Vita half marathon over 21.098, the Garmin 10 kilometres and a 200-metre kids' run. Which day you travel for depends on the distance, as the shorter races and the main marathon are split across the weekend.

Not published as of 2026-10-19. The official site confirms the two race days, 16 and 17 October 2027, and the four distances, but gives no entry fee and no statement of what a place includes. Those appear with registration.
