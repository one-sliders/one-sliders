About the event

Oslo Maraton puts five races through the city centre on one September Saturday. The marathon starts at 09:00, the half marathon at 13:00 and the 10 km known as 10 for Grete at 16:20, with an Oslotriple for anyone running all three on the same day. Entry is 1,350 kroner for the marathon and the course closes six hours after the gun.

Five races share one September Saturday in the city centre: the marathon from 09:00, the half marathon from 13:00, the 10 km called 10 for Grete from 16:20, a 10 km wheelchair race, and the Oslotriple for anyone running marathon, half and 10 km on the same day. The 10 for Grete is named after Grete Waitz, and the triple is the reason the day is built back to front, with the longest race first.

Entry is priced per distance: 1,350 kroner for the marathon, 2,700 for the Oslotriple, 800 for 10 for Grete and 300 for the 10 km wheelchair race. On top of the entry an administration fee and a one-day licence are added at sign-up, and runners who hold an annual athletics licence do not pay the latter. The course closes six hours after the marathon start, with three and a half hours for the half and one hour 45 for the 10 km.
