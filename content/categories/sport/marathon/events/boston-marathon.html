About the event

The Boston Marathon is the historic Patriots' Day road race from Hopkinton to Boston. Its point-to-point course, Heartbreak Hill and Boylston Street finish make it a defining event in the city's sporting calendar.

Tickets

Entry is by time qualification: runners must have run a certified marathon inside their age and gender standard, then apply in the B.A.A.'s registration window. Hitting the standard buys the right to apply, not a place — the field is capped and the fastest qualifiers are taken first.
