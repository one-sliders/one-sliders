About the event

The course leaves Ostermalm and takes in Vasastan, Kungsholmen past the City Hall, Gamla stan and the Royal Palace, Djurgarden and Sodermalm, climbing about 183 metres in total. Entries sold 21,000 places in two weeks when they opened, with a further 4,000 held for a later window.

The finish is the draw: runners come off the street and onto the cinder track inside the 1912 Olympic Stadium. The course starts at noon from Ostermalm and loops through Vasastan, Kungsholmen past the City Hall, Gamla stan and the Royal Palace, Djurgarden and Sodermalm, with about 183 metres of climbing. The minimum age is 18, so anyone born in 2009 or earlier can enter.
