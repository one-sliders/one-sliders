About the event

The Berlin Marathon is a major autumn road race on a fast city course that finishes near the Brandenburg Gate. It is one of the Abbott World Marathon Majors and attracts elite fields alongside tens of thousands of recreational runners.

Tickets

Places are allocated by lottery, as at every Abbott World Marathon Major. The draw opens in the autumn roughly a year before race day; charity and tour-operator places are the routes left once the lottery has been drawn.
