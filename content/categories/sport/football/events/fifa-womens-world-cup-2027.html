About the event

The FIFA Women's World Cup is the pinnacle of women's football and one of the fastest-growing events in world sport, breaking attendance and viewing records with each edition. The United States are the most successful nation with four titles. Brazil hosts in 2027 - the first time the tournament is held in South America - from 24 June to 25 July.
