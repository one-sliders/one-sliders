About UEFA European Championship

The UEFA European Championship - the Euros - is the continental championship of European men's football and, after the World Cup, the most-watched national-team tournament on earth. Spain are the record winners with four titles. Euro 2028 is co-hosted by the United Kingdom and Ireland.

Next editionUEFA European Championship

Euro 2028 runs across June and into July, co-hosted by England, Scotland, Wales, Northern Ireland and the Republic of Ireland. Twenty-four teams compete across host cities including London, Manchester, Glasgow, Cardiff and Dublin.
