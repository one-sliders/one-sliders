About the match

The UEFA Champions League final is the showpiece of European club football and the most-watched annual sporting event in the world. A single match at a neutral stadium decides the champion of Europe, contested by the two clubs that survive a season-long knockout of the continent's best. Real Madrid are the record winners.

What’s at stake
  • The European Cup — the trophy Real Madrid have lifted a record 15 times
  • Paris Saint-Germain arrive as back-to-back champions after Munich 2025 and Budapest 2026
  • The winner qualifies for the UEFA Super Cup and the FIFA Club World Cup

Travel tips
  • Arrive at the ground well before kick-off — with 70,692 inside, gates are busiest in the final two hours.
  • Take public transport: Metro Line 7 stops right by the stadium and roads around it close on match day.
  • Keep your ticket and ID ready — entry checks at UEFA finals match names to tickets.
