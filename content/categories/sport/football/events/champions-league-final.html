About this event

The 2026/27 UEFA Champions League ends at Atlético de Madrid’s Estadio Metropolitano on Saturday 5 June 2027. It will be the ground’s second final after Liverpool beat Tottenham there in 2019, and Madrid’s sixth European Cup or Champions League final. The final is a one-match decider between the surviving clubs from UEFA’s league-phase and knockout competition; the teams will not be known until the season is played.
