About the event

Norway Cup is the world's largest youth football tournament: about 2,000 teams from 50-60 nations, ages 6 to 19, and some 5,500 matches in one week on the pitches of Ekebergsletta in Oslo. 31 July to 7 August 2027. Admission is free for spectators, and so is the opening show. Run by Baekkelagets Sportsklub since 1972.

5,500 matches in eight days, all free to watch, on one open field above the Oslofjord. Teams come from 50-60 nations, from six-year-olds to 19s, and Ekebergsletta turns into a tent city of flags and samba drums. The opening show on the first evening is free too. For a spectator it is the biggest grassroots football week in the world, and the view over the fjord from the fields is part of the deal.

About 2,000 club teams in age classes from 6 to 19, boys and girls, plus adapted football. Each class plays groups and knockouts to a final on the last day; the 'best club' award has gone to KFUM Oslo five times since 2016. Alumni include Erling Haaland's father Alf Inge, Ole Gunnar Solskjaer, John Carew, Ada Hegerberg and Caroline Graham Hansen.
