About the event

The Norwegian Football Cup final has been played at Ullevaal Stadion, capacity 27,182, since 1948, and it is the one day in the Norwegian season when a third-tier club can still be on the pitch. Lillestrom beat Bodo/Glimt to take the 2025 trophy, and Fredrikstad won the 2024 final on penalties for a joint-record twelfth title. The 2027 final is on 29 May.

The cup final has been played at Ullevaal Stadion since 1948, and the 27,182-seat national stadium is sold to two club ends rather than a neutral crowd. Recent finals have been tight: Fredrikstad beat Molde on penalties in 2024 after a goalless draw, for a joint-record twelfth title, and Lillestrom beat Bodo/Glimt in 2025. The 2027 final is on 29 May.

The finalists are not known until the semi-finals are played, which is part of how the day works: the cup is open to clubs from every tier, so the pairing can be two Eliteserien sides or a top club against a lower-division one. What is at stake beyond the trophy is a European place. Molde and Bodo/Glimt have met in two of the last six finals, and France has no equivalent - the Norwegian final is a single match, no replay.
