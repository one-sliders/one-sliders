About this event

Brazil will host the first FIFA Women’s World Cup in South America when the tenth edition runs from 24 June to 25 July 2027. Thirty-two teams will play 64 matches in Belo Horizonte, Brasília, Fortaleza, Porto Alegre, Recife, Rio de Janeiro, Salvador and São Paulo. Spain arrive as reigning champions after their 1–0 final win over England in Sydney in 2023, while the United States remain the competition’s most successful team with four titles.
