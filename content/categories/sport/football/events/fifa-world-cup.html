About this event

The FIFA World Cup is football's quadrennial pinnacle; the centenary 2030 tournament is shared by Morocco, Spain and Portugal, with three opening matches in Argentina, Uruguay and Paraguay honouring the first 1930 finals. It fields 48 teams.

Tips

TransportPlan from the match city, not the host country

The six-country format makes a single transport plan unreliable. Check FIFA's confirmed fixture and stadium details before buying intercity travel.

Event dayKeep the official match details with you
