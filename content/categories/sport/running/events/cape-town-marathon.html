About this event

The Cape Town Marathon is South Africa's leading city marathon and one of the most scenic road races on the international calendar. The course runs through the city bowl and out along the Atlantic seaboard with Table Mountain and Devil's Peak in view for much of the route, on a flat and fast profile that contrasts with South Africa's better-known ultra-distance events. It holds World Athletics Gold Label status, the governing body's top tier of road-race certification, which requires an elite field of a defined international standard, drug testing, and accredited course measurement. The race has also built its identity around sustainability, working toward reduced-waste operations and carbon-conscious logistics rather than treating them as an afterthought. For visiting runners the timing matters: October falls in the Cape's spring, before peak summer heat and the height of the tourist season. The 2026 edition is scheduled for 18 October.

AirportCape Town International Airport (CPT)

Hotels

Recommended areasGreen Point; V&A Waterfront; City Bowl; Sea Point

The start and finish are in Green Point, beside the stadium and the urban park. Green Point, the V&A Waterfront and Sea Point are all within walking or short-taxi distance of the line; the City Bowl is a little further but central.

Tips

TransportStay in Green Point and walk to the start

Road closures along the Atlantic seaboard and through the city bowl begin early on race morning, and the coastal road carries much of the route, so driving between suburbs is slow until the course reopens.
