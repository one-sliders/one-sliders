About the event

New Year's Eve in Madrid is run rather than waited out: the 62nd San Silvestre Vallecana goes from the Santiago Bernabeu across the city to the Estadio de Vallecas on 31 December 2026. The popular race starts at 16:55 and is open from age 16, and the international race follows at 19:55. Around 42,000 people ran the popular race in 2025. Entry is priced by bib number: 27 euros to bib 10,000, 29 euros to 20,000 and 31 euros beyond.
