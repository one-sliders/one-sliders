Rome's Diamond League meeting falls on 27 May 2027, the fifth stop of the season after Doha, Shanghai, Xiamen and before Rabat. World Athletics published the calendar on 13 October 2026. The date sits about three weeks earlier than the 2026 edition, which was held on 4 June.

The Golden Gala Pietro Mennea is Rome's Diamond League evening, and in 2027 it falls on 27 May - the fifth of fifteen legs, after Doha, Shanghai and Xiamen and before Rabat. World Athletics published the calendar on 13 October 2026. It sits about three weeks earlier than the 2026 edition, which was held on 4 June.
