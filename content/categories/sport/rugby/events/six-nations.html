About the event

The Six Nations is the northern hemisphere's premier international rugby union championship, contested each year by England, France, Ireland, Italy, Scotland and Wales. A round-robin over five weekends, it carries traditions like the Grand Slam, the Triple Crown and the Calcutta Cup, and packs out stadiums across Europe.
