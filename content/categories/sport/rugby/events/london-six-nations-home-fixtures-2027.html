About the event

England play three home matches at Twickenham in the 2027 Six Nations. France come first on Sunday 14 February with a 15:10 GMT kick-off, Italy follow on Saturday 20 February at 16:40, and the Calcutta Cup against Scotland closes the home programme on Saturday 13 March, also at 16:40. The championship itself runs from 5 February to 13 March; England's away rounds are Ireland in Dublin and Wales in Cardiff.

England have three home rounds in 2027 and they are not evenly matched. France arrive first, on Sunday 14 February at 15:10, as champions: they took the 2026 title, their eighth, which put them clear of England as the most successful side in the championship. Italy follow on Saturday 20 February at 16:40. The Calcutta Cup against Scotland closes the home programme on Saturday 13 March, also at 16:40, and it is the last match weekend of the whole tournament.

There is no straightforward public sale for Twickenham internationals. The RFU distributes tickets through registered rugby clubs, so members apply on their own club's form through a designated club contact, paying a non-refundable admin fee of GBP 5 per ticket at the point of application. If the club's allocation covers you, the RFU emails an invitation to accept and pay by card. Nothing is posted: Six Nations tickets are issued on the Twickenham App, so the phone is the ticket. Club allocations are limited and demand routinely exceeds them.
