About the event

The Rugby League World Cup is the pinnacle of international rugby league - the 13-a-side code strongest in Australia, England, New Zealand and the Pacific. Australia have dominated it like almost no team in any sport, winning the vast majority of tournaments held since 1954. This page covers the championship-deciding final.
