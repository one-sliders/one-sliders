About the event

The NRL Grand Final decides the premiership of Australia's National Rugby League and is one of the largest single-day events on the Australian sporting calendar. The premiership traces back to the New South Wales Rugby League competition first played in 1908, making it one of the oldest continuous professional rugby league championships in the world. The match has been staged in Sydney's Olympic Park since 1999, and the winning club receives the Provan-Summons Trophy, whose image of two exhausted opponents — one from each side — leaning on each other after a 1963 match became the competition's defining photograph. Grand Final day is built as a full programme rather than a single fixture, with the women's premiership decider and supporting matches played alongside it. Tickets are heavily contested and the two competing clubs are not known until the preliminary finals a week earlier. The 2026 Grand Final is scheduled for 4 October.

Coming years

2027

More than the date is open: Sydney's hosting agreement expired after the 2026 decider and the NRL has not secured a 2027 venue. Chief executive Peter V'Landys has said Sydney is no guarantee and that Brisbane is commercially better for the game, with NSW still negotiating. The league intends to sell the Grand Final year by year rather than on a long deal. Countdown sites list 3 October 2027, which matches the usual window but has no NRL behind it.
