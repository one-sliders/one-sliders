About the event

Ireland have only two home rounds in 2027 and both are Friday-night 20:10 kick-offs at the Aviva Stadium. England open the tournament in Dublin on 5 February, and France close Ireland's campaign on 13 March, the final weekend. Two home matches rather than three means the Dublin weekends are the scarce ones in this championship.
