About the event

Sao Paulo Fashion Week, running since 1996, is Latin America's largest fashion event and the main stage for Brazilian design internationally. It shows across multiple venues in the city and has been a consistent showcase for the country's use of vibrant colour, tropical prints and diverse casting on the runway.
