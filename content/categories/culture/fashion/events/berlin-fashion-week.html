About the event

Berlin Fashion Week has two editions in the 2027 calendar, and the winter one comes first: Autumn/Winter 27 runs from 29 January to 1 February 2027, with Spring/Summer 27 following on 2 to 5 July 2027. Four days each. The shows are spread across venues rather than gathered on one site, so the schedule decides the city district more than the other way round.

Berlin runs two fashion weeks inside the 2027 calendar: Autumn/Winter 27 on 29 January to 1 February, and Spring/Summer 27 on 2 to 5 July. Four days each. The January week sits in the quiet part of the international calendar, which is why the Berlin names get attention here that they would not get in Paris - the city's own Design Talents selection lists MALAIKARAISS, TASKIN GOEC, MARIUSZ PRZYBYLSKI, Martin Quad, KOLYA BOGATYREV, Fruche, MARIE-LOUISE MULLER and BARRAGAN.

The shows are spread across venues in the city rather than gathered on one site, which decides the district more than the hotel does. Berlin Fashion Week publishes its Official Schedule per season, and public access is set show by show rather than for the week as a whole, so the schedule is what to read before travelling.
