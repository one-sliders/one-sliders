About this event

The Mass During the Night is celebrated by the Pope in St Peter's Basilica on Christmas Eve and broadcast to a worldwide audience. Attendance is free but requires tickets issued by the Prefecture of the Papal Household, and demand far exceeds the basilica's capacity.
