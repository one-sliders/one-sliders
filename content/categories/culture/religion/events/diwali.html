About the event

Diwali, the Festival of Lights, is the most important festival in Hinduism and is also celebrated by Jains, Sikhs and some Buddhists. Marked by rows of oil lamps (diyas), fireworks, sweets and family gatherings, it symbolises the victory of light over darkness and good over evil. It is a public holiday across India and in many countries with large South Asian communities.
