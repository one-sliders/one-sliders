About the festival

The Berlin International Film Festival - the Berlinale - is one of the 'big three' European festivals alongside Cannes and Venice, held each February. Its top prize, the Golden Bear, is among cinema's most coveted, and the festival is known for its political edge and huge public audience.

Public tickets are sold online through the official Berlinale shop in the run-up to each screening. The 2026 edition set a ticket-sales record.
