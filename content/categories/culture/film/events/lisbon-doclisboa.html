About the event

The 24th DocLisboa runs 15 to 25 October 2026 across two houses: Culturgest, with both the Pequeno Auditorio and the Auditorio Emilio Rui Vilar, and Sala 3 at Cinema Sao Jorge. Eleven days of documentary in the middle of the city, with the festival office at Casa do Cinema on Rua da Rosa.

Two addresses carry the festival. Culturgest runs two auditoriums, the Pequeno Auditorio and the Auditorio Emilio Rui Vilar, and Cinema Sao Jorge contributes Sala 3. The festival office sits apart from both, at Casa do Cinema on Rua da Rosa 277.

Not published as of 2026-10-20. The festival's own pages give the dates, the edition and the venues, but no ticket prices, no pass and no accreditation terms for the 24th edition.
