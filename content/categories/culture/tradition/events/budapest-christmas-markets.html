About the event

The Basilica market opens earliest and closes latest: 13 November 2026 to 1 January 2027 on Szent Istvan Square, in front of the basilica itself. That seven-week run is the long one in Budapest, and it carries the New Year where the other markets stop before it.

The market runs continuously from 13 November 2026 to 1 January 2027 on Szent Istvan Square. Its setting is the point: the stalls stand in the square directly in front of St Stephen's Basilica, which is also the surface the light projections are shown on.
