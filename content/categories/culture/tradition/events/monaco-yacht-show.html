About the event

Four days in Port Hercule, 22 to 25 September 2027, with more than 560 exhibitors, around 120 superyachts moored for sale, charter and viewing, and some 50 tenders alongside them. The show takes over the whole harbour rather than a hall, which is why the quaysides around the port are the exhibition floor and the yachts themselves are the stands.
