About the event

Madrid's patron saint festival closes on 16 May 2027 - esMadrid gives that end date and puts the start only at 'early May'. The city's own listing leaves the 2027 venues blank, which means the programme is not out either. What is known is the shape: concerts and parades in the squares, the religious events at the saint's chapel, and the bullfighting season at Las Ventas.
