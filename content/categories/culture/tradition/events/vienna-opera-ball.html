About the event

The 69th Vienna Opera Ball opens at 20:15 on 4 February 2027. For one night the opera house is rebuilt as a ballroom: nine floors, five dance floors, 20 bars and buffets and 16 music genres running until morning. One evening, one address - which makes the date the only thing to plan around.

Doors and opening are set for 20:15 on 4 February 2027. The house is rebuilt for the night across nine floors, with five dance floors and 16 music genres running in parallel and 20 bars and buffets between them - so the evening is navigated by floor rather than followed as a single programme.

Not published as of 2026-10-19 in price terms. The ball is a ticketed event open to buyers rather than an invitation-only occasion, and the official site routes to a Tickets and Info section, but it carries no prices or categories on the main page. The dress code is likewise not stated there.
