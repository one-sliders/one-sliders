About the event

The Winter Lights Festival runs 4 to 7 February 2027 across all six municipalities of the capital area, with more than 150 events and free admission. Two of them define it: Museum Night and Pool Night, when the city's museums and swimming pools stay open late. The Light Trail runs alongside. The full programme is published closer to the date.

More than 150 events over four days, 4 to 7 February 2027, spread across all six municipalities of the capital area - and admission is free. The festival runs 4 to 7 February 2027 and is built from three named strands - Museum Night, Pool Night and the Light Trail - plus more than 150 individual events across the six capital-area municipalities. The full schedule, including which night each strand falls on, is published closer to the festival.
