About the event

Nine days of livestock, regional food halls and farm machinery in the middle of Paris: the Salon International de l'Agriculture fills Paris Expo Porte de Versailles from 27 February to 7 March 2027, open 09:00 to 19:00. It is the largest agricultural fair in France and a fixture of the political calendar as much as the farming one.
