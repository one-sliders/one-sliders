About the event

Oslo Pride runs for eleven days in late June and ends with the parade through the city centre on the final Saturday. Recent parades have drawn around 50,000 marchers, with about 90,000 in 2026, and the organisers count up to 300,000 spectators along the route. Pride Park at Kontraskjaeret is the festival's open-air base for the week.

Oslo Pride is eleven days rather than a single parade day, and the city centre is the venue. Pride Park at Kontraskjaeret, under Akershus Fortress, is the open-air base through the week, and the parade on the closing Saturday is the largest single gathering in Norway: about 90,000 people marched in 2026 and organisers count up to 300,000 spectators along the route through the centre.

Watching costs nothing and needs no ticket: the parade runs through public streets and the crowd along the route is counted in the hundreds of thousands. Anyone can also march rather than watch, which is the point of the day. Individual club nights and ticketed concerts in the programme are separate, but the parade itself and Pride Park are open to anyone who turns up.
