About the event

Bern's onion market falls on Monday 23 November 2026 - always the fourth Monday in November. The official hours are 06:00 to 18:00, but the first sales start at about 04:00, and that early hour is the point: the stalls fill the lanes of the upper old town, Waisenhausplatz and Bundesplatz with onion plaits, wreaths and figures before the city is properly awake. Entry is free.
