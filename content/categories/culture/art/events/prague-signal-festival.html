About the event

Four nights of light art in Prague, 14 to 17 October 2027. The format splits in two: the installations on the public routes - 13 of them across 17 locations in the last edition, on the Dejvice and Centrum routes - are free to walk, while the indoor Signal INSIDE programme is ticketed. Note that the site's own FAQ still carries the previous edition's dates.

Not published as of 2026-10-19. The 2027 routes and nightly hours are not out - the site's FAQ still carries the previous edition's dates, which is worth knowing before relying on anything there. What is confirmed is the four-night window, 14 to 17 October 2027.
