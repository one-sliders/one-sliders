About the fair

International art fair showcasing modern and contemporary art through leading galleries. It is staged in Basel, Switzerland. Founded in 1970 by the Basel gallerists Trudl Bruckner, Balz Hilt and Ernst Beyeler, it opened with 90 galleries from 10 countries and now turns Messe Basel into a temporary museum of more than 200 selected galleries.

Tickets

Day and multi-day tickets are sold online through Art Basel — preview days (16–17 June) are for invited guests; public days run 17–20 June.

Travel tips
  • The fair draws over 90,000 visitors — book Basel hotels months ahead or stay across the border in France or Germany.
  • Basel hotel guests get a free public-transport pass covering trams to the Messe.
  • Satellite fairs like Liste run the same week — plan more than one day.
