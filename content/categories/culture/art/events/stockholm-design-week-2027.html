About the event

Stockholm Design Week runs 8 to 14 February 2027. It is organised by the Stockholm Furniture Fair but happens outside it: the week fills the city with exhibitions and open showrooms rather than gathering everything in one hall. The fair's own dates and the number of participating events are published separately.

Not published as of 2026-10-19. Stockholm Design Week has announced 8 to 14 February 2027 and nothing else: no event list, no showroom opening hours and no fair dates alongside it. Which days are busiest therefore cannot be stated for this edition.
