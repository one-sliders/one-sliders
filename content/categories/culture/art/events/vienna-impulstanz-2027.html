About the event

ImPulsTanz runs for a full month, 8 July to 8 August 2027, and it is built on four pillars rather than a stage programme alone: international performances with world and Austrian premieres, hundreds of workshop classes open to beginners as well as professionals, free outdoor Public Moves classes, and the Socal evening programme. A visit can be as a dancer or as an audience.
