About the event

The 25th anniversary London Design Festival runs 18 to 26 September 2027. It is a city-wide festival by construction: the last edition was organised into 11 design districts, each with its own programme, so the week is navigated by neighbourhood rather than by venue. The 2027 district list follows with the programme.

Not published as of 2026-10-19. The 2027 district list and programme are not out, so which days and areas fill up cannot be stated. For scale: the previous edition ran 11 design districts across nine days, 12 to 20 September 2026.
