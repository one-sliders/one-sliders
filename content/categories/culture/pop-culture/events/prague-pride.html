About the event

Prague Pride takes the week of 2 to 8 August 2027, with the Pride Parade on Saturday 7 August. The week is a festival rather than a single march - talks, film, concerts and the Pride Village spread across venues in the city - and the parade is the closing set piece that draws the largest crowd.

Prague Pride is a week, not a march: 2 to 8 August 2027, with the parade on Saturday 7 August. The 2026 edition ran close to 200 events across six locations in the city and moved its main site to Stvanice Island, where the parade now finishes and Pride Park is held. The route starts at the lower end of Wenceslas Square and crosses the Old Town.

The parade and the park are open and free. What is ticketed is the indoor programme - the club nights, theatre and film events spread across the week's venues - and those are sold per event rather than as a festival pass. The 2027 programme and its prices had not been published as of 20 October 2026.
