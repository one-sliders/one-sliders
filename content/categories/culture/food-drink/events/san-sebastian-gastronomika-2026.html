About the event

San Sebastian Gastronomika is one of the world's leading culinary congresses, held each autumn in the Basque city of San Sebastian - a place with more Michelin stars per head than almost anywhere on earth. It gathers many of the world's top chefs on one stage for live demonstrations, debates and the unveiling of new techniques, and has helped shape modern fine dining.
