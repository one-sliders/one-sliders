About the event

The Grammy Awards are the music industry's most prestigious honours, presented by the Recording Academy each winter in Los Angeles. From Record of the Year to dozens of genre categories, a Grammy is the defining accolade in recorded music, and the ceremony doubles as one of the year's biggest live-music broadcasts.

Nominations are scheduled for 16 November 2026; use the Recording Academy for the current broadcast and ticket context.

The 69th Grammys is the first on ABC. After more than fifty years on CBS the ceremony moves to ABC, with streaming on Hulu and Disney+, under a ten-year deal that starts with this edition. It stays at the Crypto.com Arena in downtown Los Angeles, live, on 7 February 2027.

No host and no performers had been announced as of 21 October 2026, and the producer was not confirmed either. The voting calendar is set: nominations on 16 November 2026, final-round voting from 10 December to 7 January.
