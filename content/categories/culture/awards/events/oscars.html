About the event

The Academy Awards, or Oscars, are the Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences' annual film awards, first presented in 1929. The ceremony pairs competitive prizes for film, acting and craft with a global TV audience at the close of a long awards season. The 99th ceremony is set for 14 March 2027 at the Dolby Theatre in Hollywood. It is not open to the public, but awards week fills Hollywood and Los Angeles with screenings and film-culture events.

No. The Oscars is an industry ceremony with an invited audience - nominees, guests and Academy members - and no public ticket. For everyone else it is a broadcast: ABC carries the 99th ceremony live from the Dolby Theatre, and it is the second-to-last Oscars on that network before YouTube takes over global rights from 2029.

Neither the host nor the performers for the 99th ceremony had been announced as of 21 October 2026. What is set is the slot: 14 March 2027 at the Dolby Theatre, starting 19:00 Eastern - 01:00 CET on the 15th - with ABC keeping the earlier start time.

Travel tips
  • Treat the surrounding Hollywood Boulevard as a crowd-management exercise and follow the Academy’s official access advice. The ceremony starts at 7:00 pm ET on Sunday 14 March 2027, which is 00:00 CET — the night into Monday for European viewers.
  • LAX has the widest international schedule, while Hollywood Burbank is closer for selected domestic routes.
  • Arrive at least a day early because awards-week activity is spread across Los Angeles and traffic is unpredictable.
