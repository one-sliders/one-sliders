About this event

The Academy Awards, or Oscars, are the Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences' annual film awards, first presented in 1929. The ceremony pairs competitive prizes for film, acting and craft with a global TV audience at the close of a long awards season. The 99th ceremony is set for 14 March 2027 at the Dolby Theatre in Hollywood. It is not open to the public, but awards week fills Hollywood and Los Angeles with screenings and film-culture events.
