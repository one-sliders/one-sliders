About this event

The BAFTA Film Awards began with a ceremony in May 1949, which honoured The Best Years of Our Lives. BAFTA's bronze theatrical mask and marble-base trophy is a distinctive part of the ceremony. The awards recognise film work through the British Academy of Film and Television Arts rather than being a general London entertainment event.

Flights

AirportHeathrow Airport (LHR)

The Elizabeth line reaches Paddington from Heathrow; from there, use the Underground or walk toward the carnival perimeter because road closures affect local buses.

ArrivalArrive before 21 February if you need local check-in or opening-day access.

Hotels

Recommended areasNotting Hill; Paddington; Kensington; Shepherd’s Bush

Stay near a Tube or Elizabeth line station outside the densest parade streets; central-west London is easier to leave after the sound systems finish.

Tips

TransportUse the official carnival map and transport advice on the day.

Several nearby stations operate entry or exit controls during the parade, so pick your route after the organiser and Transport for London publish their final arrangements.

Event dayCarry water, wear comfortable shoes and agree a meeting point before entering the crowds.

BAFTA Film Awards is dated 21 February 2027; use that range when planning arrival, tickets and reminders.
