About the event

The 80th Tony Awards has no announced date. The 79th was held on Sunday 7 June 2026 at Radio City Music Hall, live on CBS and streaming on Paramount+, with nominations revealed on 5 May across 26 competitive categories and P!nk hosting. Radio City has been the Tonys' usual home since 1997, and the date for the next ceremony is normally announced in the winter.

The Tony Awards are Broadway theatre's highest honour, presented in New York for the season's best plays, musicals and performances. Named for Antoinette Perry, a win can extend a show's run and fill houses on the Great White Way.

Yes - the Tonys is held in a theatre and tickets are sold, unlike the film academies' invited-audience ceremonies. Radio City Music Hall has been the usual home since 1997, with Lincoln Center and Washington Heights used in between. The broadcast goes out live on CBS with streaming on Paramount+.
