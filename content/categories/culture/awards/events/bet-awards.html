No date has been announced for the 2027 BET Awards. The 2026 edition was held on 28 June at the Peacock Theater in Los Angeles, and the show has usually fallen in late June. Countdown sites list dates for 2027, but BET itself has confirmed neither a date nor a venue.

The BET Awards opened at the Paris Las Vegas on 19 June 2001, with Steve Harvey and Cedric the Entertainer hosting and Whitney Houston receiving the first Lifetime Achievement Award. The ceremony recognises Black achievement across music, film, television and sport. Beyoncé holds the event's record for both wins and nominations, while the show has also made its performance stage a place for tributes and political speeches.
