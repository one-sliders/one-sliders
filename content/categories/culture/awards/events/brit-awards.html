About this event

Launched in 1977, the BRIT Awards are the annual showcase for the United Kingdom's recorded-music industry. The BPI organises the ceremony, while proceeds support the BRIT Trust and its music-education and wellbeing work. The 2027 ceremony returns to Manchester's Co-op Live for the second year of the awards' move outside London.
