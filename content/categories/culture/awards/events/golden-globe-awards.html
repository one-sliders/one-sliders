About the event

The Golden Globe Awards honour the best in both film and television at a glamorous January ceremony in Beverly Hills, traditionally seen as an early bellwether for the Oscars. Known for their loose, champagne-fuelled atmosphere, the Globes are one of Hollywood's most-watched award shows.

CBS and Paramount+ carry the ceremony in the United States; check Golden Globes and broadcaster information for final access details.

The 84th Golden Globes runs live from the Beverly Hilton on 10 January 2027, 20:00 to 23:00 Eastern - 02:00 to 05:00 CET on the 11th - and goes out live on both American coasts rather than tape-delayed on the west. Nikki Glaser hosts again. CBS carries it with a simulcast on Paramount+.
