About the event

Over 10,000 square metres of ice are laid across Rathausplatz from 22 January to 7 March 2027 - six and a half weeks, which makes this a season rather than an event. Booking online takes 10 per cent off the ticket, and Mondays are half price for Vorteilsclub members. Note that the site's own opening-times section still carries the previous season's end date.
