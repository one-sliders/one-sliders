About the event

Money20/20 Europe returns to the RAI in Amsterdam on 8 to 10 June 2027. It is the continent's fintech meeting rather than a consumer show: the 2026 edition counted more than 7,500 attendees and over 2,300 attending companies, and the three days turn on scheduled meetings between banks, payment firms and investors. No attendance figures for 2027 have been published yet.
