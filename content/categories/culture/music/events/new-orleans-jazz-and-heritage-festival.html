About this event

The New Orleans Jazz & Heritage Festival, widely called Jazz Fest, is the city’s annual celebration of Louisiana music, food, art and cultural heritage. The 2027 edition is scheduled across two four-day weekends: 22–25 April and 29 April–2 May at the Fair Grounds Race Course. Its name is broader than jazz alone: the programme brings local traditions into the same festival landscape as major touring artists, while food, crafts and cultural presentations are part of the experience rather than side attractions. The organiser’s food programme includes Creole and Cajun traditions alongside contemporary New Orleans cooking and dishes shaped by Louisiana’s international influences. The 2027 performer announcement is not yet published; the official visitor guide says it is normally released closer to the event. Visitors should therefore use the dates and venue for planning, then return to the official festival channels for the final daily programme and ticket details.
