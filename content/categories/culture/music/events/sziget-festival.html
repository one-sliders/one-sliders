About Sziget Festival

Sziget is one of Europe's biggest music festivals, held every August on Obuda Island in the middle of Budapest. Nicknamed the 'Island of Freedom', its week-long, camp-on-site format and hugely international crowd make it as much a cultural gathering as a concert series.
