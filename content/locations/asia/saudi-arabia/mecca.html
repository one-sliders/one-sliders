Mecca city overview

Mecca works best as a base in Saudi Arabia when you anchor days around Masjid al-Haram, Kaaba and Abraj Al Bait. Use those sights to understand the city's history, street life and visitor rhythm. Mecca International Airport handles the main arrival flow, so hotel choice should balance the centre, transfer time and day-trip plans.
