Baghdad city overview

Baghdad works best as a base in Iraq when you anchor days around Al-Mutanabbi Street, National Museum of Iraq and Al-Shaheed Monument. Use those sights to understand the city's history, street life and visitor rhythm. Baghdad International handles the main arrival flow, so hotel choice should balance the centre, transfer time and day-trip plans.
