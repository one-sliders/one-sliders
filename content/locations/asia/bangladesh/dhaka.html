Dhaka city overview

Dhaka works best as a base in Bangladesh when you anchor days around Lalbagh Fort, Ahsan Manzil and Dhakeshwari Temple. Use those sights to understand the city's history, street life and visitor rhythm. Dhaka Hazrat Shahjalal handles the main arrival flow, so hotel choice should balance the centre, transfer time and day-trip plans.
