Sanaa city overview

Sanaa works best as a base in Yemen when you anchor days around Old City of Sanaa, Bab al-Yemen and Great Mosque of Sanaa. Use those sights to understand the city's history, street life and visitor rhythm. Sanaa International Airport handles the main arrival flow, so hotel choice should balance the centre, transfer time and day-trip plans.
