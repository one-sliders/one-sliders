Amman city overview

Amman is a Jordan base best planned around named sights rather than a generic stopover. Start with Amman Citadel, add Roman Theater Amman, and use Rainbow Street to understand the city's history, street life and visitor rhythm. Amman Queen Alia handles the main arrival flow, so hotel choice should balance the centre, transfer time and day-trip plans.
