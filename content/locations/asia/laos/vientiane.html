Vientiane city overview

Vientiane works best as a base in Laos when you anchor days around Pha That Luang, Patuxai and Wat Si Saket. Use those sights to understand the city's history, street life and visitor rhythm. Vientiane Wattay handles the main arrival flow, so hotel choice should balance the centre, transfer time and day-trip plans.
