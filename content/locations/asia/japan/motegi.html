Motegi city overview

Motegi works best as a base in Japan when you anchor days around Mobility Resort Motegi, Honda Collection Hall and Twin Ring Motegi Road Course. Use those sights to understand the city's history, street life and visitor rhythm. Motegi International Airport handles the main arrival flow, so hotel choice should balance the centre, transfer time and day-trip plans.
