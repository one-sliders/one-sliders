Denpasar city overview

Denpasar works best as a base in Indonesia when you anchor days around Bajra Sandhi Monument, Puputan Square and Bali Museum. Use those sights to understand the city's history, street life and visitor rhythm. Bali Denpasar handles the main arrival flow, so hotel choice should balance the centre, transfer time and day-trip plans.
