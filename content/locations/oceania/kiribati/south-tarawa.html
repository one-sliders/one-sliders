South Tarawa city overview

South Tarawa works best as a base in Kiribati when you anchor days around Ambo Island, Parliament House of Kiribati and Betio War Relics. Use those sights to understand the city's history, street life and visitor rhythm. Bonriki International Airport handles the main arrival flow, so hotel choice should balance the centre, transfer time and day-trip plans.

City history

1895South Tarawa became the seat of administration in the Gilbert Islands, beginning its role as the political centre of the atoll chain.
1906Ocean Island served as colonial headquarters from 1906, while Tarawa remained central to Gilbertese settlement and lagoon travel.
1943Betio on Tarawa was the site of the Battle of Tarawa during World War II, one of the Pacific War's major amphibious battles.
1946After wartime disruption, the colonial headquarters returned to Tarawa, strengthening South Tarawa's administrative role.
1979Kiribati became independent, and South Tarawa became the capital area of the new republic.
TodayModern South Tarawa concentrates government, port activity, dense settlement and climate-risk planning on a narrow coral atoll.
