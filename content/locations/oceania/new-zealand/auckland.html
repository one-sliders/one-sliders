Auckland city overview

Auckland is a New Zealand base best planned around named sights rather than a generic stopover. Start with Sky Tower Auckland, add Auckland War Memorial Museum, and use Auckland Harbour Bridge to understand the city's history, street life and visitor rhythm. Auckland International Airport handles the main arrival flow, so hotel choice should balance the centre, transfer time and day-trip plans.
