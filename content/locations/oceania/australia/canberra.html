Canberra city overview

Canberra is a Australia base best planned around named sights rather than a generic stopover. Start with Australian Parliament House, add Australian War Memorial, and use National Gallery of Australia to understand the city's history, street life and visitor rhythm. Canberra International Airport handles the main arrival flow, so hotel choice should balance the centre, transfer time and day-trip plans.

Civic roleThe Canberry or Nganbra clan lived mostly around Sullivan's Creek and had ceremonial grounds at the base of Galambary (Black Mountain), while the Pialligo clan had land around what is now Canberra Airport.
