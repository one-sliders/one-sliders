Sydney city overview

Sydney works best as a base in Australia when you anchor days around Sydney Opera House, Sydney Harbour Bridge and Bondi Beach. Use those sights to understand the city's history, street life and visitor rhythm. Sydney Kingsford Smith handles the main arrival flow, so hotel choice should balance the centre, transfer time and day-trip plans.
