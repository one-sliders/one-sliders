Calgary city overview

Calgary works best as a base in Canada when you anchor days around Calgary Tower, Calgary Stampede and Bow River Pathway. Use those sights to understand the city's history, street life and visitor rhythm. Calgary International Airport handles the main arrival flow, so hotel choice should balance the centre, transfer time and day-trip plans.
