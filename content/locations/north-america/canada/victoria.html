Victoria city overview

Victoria works best as a base in Canada when you anchor days around Inner Harbour, Butchart Gardens and Royal BC Museum. Use those sights to understand the city's history, street life and visitor rhythm. Seychelles International handles the main arrival flow, so hotel choice should balance the centre, transfer time and day-trip plans.
