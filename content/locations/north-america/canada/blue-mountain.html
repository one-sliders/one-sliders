Blue Mountain city overview

Blue Mountain rises off the Niagara Escarpment above Georgian Bay, a couple of hours north of Toronto, and it is Ontario's busiest ski hill. The operator counts 43 runs across 364 acres, with snowmaking on 360 of them and 30 trails lit for night skiing - five high-speed six-seater chairs carry most of the traffic, alongside a quad, a triple and four surface lifts.

Book against high season and weekends - this is Toronto's nearest big hill. The village is ski-in; Collingwood needs a short drive.

Stay overview

The pedestrian Blue Mountain Village sits at the foot of the lifts; Collingwood is the town ten minutes away on Georgian Bay, with more to eat and cheaper beds.
