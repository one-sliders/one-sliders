Edmonton city overview

Edmonton works best as a base in Canada when you anchor days around North Saskatchewan River Valley, West Edmonton Mall and Ice District. Use those sights to understand the city's history, street life and visitor rhythm. Edmonton International Airport handles the main arrival flow, so hotel choice should balance the centre, transfer time and day-trip plans.
