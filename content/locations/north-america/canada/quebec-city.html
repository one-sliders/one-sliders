Quebec City city overview

Quebec City is a Canada base best planned around named sights rather than a generic stopover. Start with Old Quebec, add Chateau Frontenac, and use Petit-Champlain to understand the city's history, street life and visitor rhythm. Quebec City International Airport handles the main arrival flow, so hotel choice should balance the centre, transfer time and day-trip plans.
