Sun Peaks city overview

Sun Peaks is 4,400 acres across three mountains, which the resort states makes it Canada's second largest ski area. Fifty-eight per cent of the 144 trails are graded intermediate, the village street has runs coming down through it, and the season runs 21 November 2026 to 4 April 2027.
