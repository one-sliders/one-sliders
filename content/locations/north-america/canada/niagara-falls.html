Niagara Falls city overview

Niagara Falls is a Canada base best planned around named sights rather than a generic stopover. Start with Horseshoe Falls, add Journey Behind the Falls, and use Clifton Hill to understand the city's history, street life and visitor rhythm. Niagara Falls International Airport handles the main arrival flow, so hotel choice should balance the centre, transfer time and day-trip plans.
