Niagara Falls city overview

Niagara Falls works best as a base in Canada when you anchor days around Horseshoe Falls, Journey Behind the Falls and Clifton Hill. Use those sights to understand the city's history, street life and visitor rhythm. Niagara Falls International Airport handles the main arrival flow, so hotel choice should balance the centre, transfer time and day-trip plans.
