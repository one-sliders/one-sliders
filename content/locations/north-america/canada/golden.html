Golden city overview

Kicking Horse rises 1,315 metres above Golden in the Columbia Valley, and 60% of its terrain is graded advanced or expert. The resort counts over 85 inbound chutes, the Golden Eagle Express gondola runs 3,413 metres in twelve minutes, and the summit stations record 750 cm of snow a year.
