Whistler city overview

Whistler is two mountains on one lift pass, linked at the top by the PEAK 2 PEAK Gondola. Together they hold 8,171 acres and over 200 trails, the vertical runs to 1,609 metres on Blackcomb, and Peak to Creek drops 11 kilometres from the top of Whistler Mountain to Creekside. The summit has averaged 10.4 metres of snow a year over the last ten seasons.

Stay overview

Where you sleep in Whistler decides which gondola you walk to. Whistler Village has both mountain gondolas and the PEAK 2 PEAK; the Upper Village sits at Blackcomb's foot; Creekside is the original 1966 base with its own gondola and easier parking; and Function Junction is the cheap end of the valley, on the highway south.

Travel tips

One pass, two mountains

The PEAK 2 PEAK Gondola crosses between Whistler and Blackcomb at altitude, so you can change mountain without skiing down.

Which mountain

Blackcomb is graded 30% advanced against Whistler's 25%, and both are 55% intermediate.

Longest run

Peak to Creek runs 11 kilometres from the top of Whistler Mountain down to Creekside.

Eating up the hill

There are 18 restaurants and bars on the two mountains, seating 6,972, so lunch does not mean skiing back to the village.
