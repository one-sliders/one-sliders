Ottawa city overview

Ottawa works best as a base in Canada when you anchor days around Parliament Hill, Rideau Canal and ByWard Market. Use those sights to understand the city's history, street life and visitor rhythm. Ottawa International Airport handles the main arrival flow, so hotel choice should balance the centre, transfer time and day-trip plans.
