Revelstoke city overview

Revelstoke has 1,713 metres of vertical, which the resort states is the greatest in North America, served by four lifts over 3,121 acres. The Last Spike runs 15.2 kilometres from Sub Peak to a village at 512 metres, and the mountain takes 10.5 metres of snow in an average year.
