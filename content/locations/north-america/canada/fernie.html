Fernie city overview

Fernie has five alpine bowls, 145 named runs and tree skiing between them, spread over more than 2,500 acres with 1,082 metres of vertical. Nine metres of snow falls in an average year and up to 37 feet in a good one, and the old brick coal town sits five kilometres down the valley.
