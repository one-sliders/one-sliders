Bridgetown city overview

Bridgetown is a Barbados base best planned around named sights rather than a generic stopover. Start with Garrison Savannah, add Parliament Buildings Bridgetown, and use Nidhe Israel Synagogue to understand the city's history, street life and visitor rhythm. Grantley Adams International Airport handles the main arrival flow, so hotel choice should balance the centre, transfer time and day-trip plans.
