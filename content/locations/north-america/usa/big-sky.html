Big Sky city overview

Lone Peak rises to 11,166 feet above Big Sky, and from the summit the resort offers 300 degrees of skiing across 5,850 acres. The village sits at 2,200 metres in Montana's Madison Range, an hour from Bozeman and an hour from Yellowstone.
