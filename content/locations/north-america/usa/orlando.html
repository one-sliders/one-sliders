Orlando city overview

Orlando works best as a base in USA when you anchor days around Walt Disney World Resort, Universal Orlando Resort and International Drive. Use those sights to understand the city's history, street life and visitor rhythm. Orlando International Airport handles the main arrival flow, so hotel choice should balance the centre, transfer time and day-trip plans.
