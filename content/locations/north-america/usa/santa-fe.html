1598In 1598, Juan de Oñate established the area as Santa Fe de Nuevo México, a province of New Spain.
1607New Mexico's second Spanish governor, Don Pedro de Peralta, however, founded a new city at the foot of the Sangre de Cristo Mountains in 1607, which he called La Villa Real de la Santa.
1610In 1610, he designated it as the capital of the province, which it has almost constantly remained, making it the oldest state capital in the United States.
1680The Pueblo people continued running New Mexico from the Palace of the Governors in Santa Fe from 1680 to 1692.
