Breckenridge city overview

Breckenridge is a Victorian mining town with 2,908 acres above it, and a third of that terrain is graded expert - a share no other resort in this batch publishes. The summit on Peak 8 reaches 12,998 feet, and the 4 O'Clock Run ends on Main Street.

Stay overview

Main Street is the reason to stay in Breckenridge rather than at a base: the 4 O'Clock Run brings you back to it, and the restaurants are there. Peak 8 and Peak 9 are the ski-in alternatives.
