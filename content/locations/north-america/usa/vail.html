Vail city overview

Vail is 5,317 acres over 278 trails, and more than half of it is graded advanced. Thirty-two lifts serve a mountain topping out at 3,527 metres, the Back Bowls run behind the front face, and Riva Ridge drops four miles from the ridge to the village.
