Palisades Tahoe city overview

Palisades Tahoe is two mountains on one pass above Lake Tahoe. The Palisades side holds 3,600 acres, 187 trails and sixteen bowls; Alpine holds 2,400 acres, 109 trails and seven. Both average 400 inches of snow a year, and the Palisades side hosted the 1960 Winter Olympics.
