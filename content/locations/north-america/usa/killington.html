Killington city overview

Killington is the largest ski area in New England: 1,509 acres and 155 trails on its own, and 1,977 acres with 213 trails counting Pico next door. The summit reaches 4,241 feet and the base sits at 1,165, which is a lot of vertical for the east coast.
