Telluride city overview

Telluride sits at the closed end of a box canyon in the San Juan Mountains, with 149 trails served by 17 lifts above it. A free gondola links the Victorian town to Mountain Village at mid-mountain, and Black Iron Bowl holds well over 200 acres of hike-to terrain on the north face.
