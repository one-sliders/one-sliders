Steamboat Springs city overview

Steamboat grew to 3,741 acres and 181 trails when Mahogany Ridge opened, adding over 650 acres of advanced and expert terrain with rock cliffs and chutes - and making it, in the resort's own words, the second largest ski resort in Colorado. The town below is a working ranching town with hot springs.
