Saint Georges city overview

Saint Georges works best as a base in Grenada when you anchor days around Carenage, Fort George Grenada and Grand Anse Beach. Use those sights to understand the city's history, street life and visitor rhythm. Maurice Bishop International Airport handles the main arrival flow, so hotel choice should balance the centre, transfer time and day-trip plans.
