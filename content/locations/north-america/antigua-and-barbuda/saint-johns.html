Worth seeing

Cathedral of St John the Divine

The Cathedral of St John the Divine's twin baroque towers dominate the skyline, rebuilt in stone after earthquakes destroyed earlier wooden churches.

Redcliffe Quay

Redcliffe Quay's restored 18th-century warehouses now hold boutiques, cafes and galleries, a pleasant walk from the cruise terminal at Heritage Quay.

Heritage Quay

Heritage Quay is the main cruise dock and duty-free shopping strip, busiest when ships are in port and quieter for an evening stroll.

Museum of Antigua and Barbuda

The Museum of Antigua and Barbuda, housed in the old courthouse, traces the island's Arawak, colonial and independence history in one compact gallery.

Fort James

Fort James guards the harbour entrance with 18th-century cannons and views over the water, a short taxi ride from the city centre.

Dickenson Bay

Dickenson Bay is the closest resort beach to St. John's, a calm stretch of sand lined with waterfront restaurants and watersports operators.

Stay overview

Base in Redcliffe Quay or Heritage Quay for an easy first St. John's stay near the harbour, restaurants and cruise terminal, then compare Dickenson Bay for a beach hotel strip or Jolly Harbour for a marina setting with villas and golf a short drive from the centre.

Hotels & areas

St. John's hotel choice comes down to setting: Redcliffe Quay and Heritage Quay suit short cruise stopovers and harbour-side dining, while Dickenson Bay offers a classic beach strip and Jolly Harbour suits families or longer stays wanting a marina and golf course nearby.

Travel tips

Best base

Stay in Redcliffe Quay or Heritage Quay for an easy first visit within walking distance of the harbour, cathedral and cruise terminal.

Getting around

Taxis and shared buses run between St. John's and the beaches; agree taxi fares before departure since meters are not standard.

Cruise days

Heritage Quay gets crowded when several cruise ships dock, so visit the cathedral and market early before the day-trip crowds arrive.

Day trips

Set aside half a day for Nelson's Dockyard and English Harbour on the south coast, one of the Caribbean's best-preserved Georgian naval sites.
