Castries city overview

Castries is a Saint Lucia base best planned around named sights rather than a generic stopover. Start with Castries Market, add Derek Walcott Square, and use Cathedral Basilica of the Immaculate Conception to understand the city's history, street life and visitor rhythm. Hewanorra International Airport handles the main arrival flow, so hotel choice should balance the centre, transfer time and day-trip plans.
