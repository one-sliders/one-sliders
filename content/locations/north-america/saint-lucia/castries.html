Castries city overview

Castries works best as a base in Saint Lucia when you anchor days around Castries Market, Derek Walcott Square and Cathedral Basilica of the Immaculate Conception. Use those sights to understand the city's history, street life and visitor rhythm. Hewanorra International Airport handles the main arrival flow, so hotel choice should balance the centre, transfer time and day-trip plans.
