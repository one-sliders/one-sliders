San Jose city overview

San Jose works best as a base in Costa Rica when you anchor days around National Theatre of Costa Rica, Pre-Columbian Gold Museum and Central Market San Jose. Use those sights to understand the city's history, street life and visitor rhythm. San Jose International Airport handles the main arrival flow, so hotel choice should balance the centre, transfer time and day-trip plans.

City history

1736Founded in 1736 by order of Cabildo de León, its objective was to concentrate the scattered inhabitants of the Aserrí Valley.
1812It was not until the enactment of the Constitution of Cádiz in 1812 that San José had its first city government.
1814On 18 October 1813, the area was first defined as a city by presbyter Florencio del Castillo, on behalf of the Spanish government, a title which was then lost in 1814 when Ferdinand VII.
1820The municipal government was restored in 1820 along with the title of city and in 1823 San José became the capital of Costa Rica.
19th centuryThe first modern urban neighborhood carries the name of its founder, the French coffee entrepreneur Monsieur Amon, and was created in the late 19th century, in line with Belle Époque contemporary architecture.
