Riga city overview

Riga is a Latvia base best planned around named sights rather than a generic stopover. Start with Riga Old Town, add House of the Blackheads, and use Riga Central Market to understand the city's history, street life and visitor rhythm. Riga International handles the main arrival flow, so hotel choice should balance the centre, transfer time and day-trip plans.
