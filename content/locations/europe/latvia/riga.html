Riga city overview

Riga works best as a base in Latvia when you anchor days around Riga Old Town, House of the Blackheads and Riga Central Market. Use those sights to understand the city's history, street life and visitor rhythm. Riga International handles the main arrival flow, so hotel choice should balance the centre, transfer time and day-trip plans.
