Alta Badia city overview

Alta Badia is a cluster of Ladin villages at the head of the Badia valley - Corvara sits at 1,568 metres - ringed by the Sella massif and the Dolomites that UNESCO lists as World Heritage. The ski area counts 54 lifts and 117 slopes, among the largest in the Dolomiti Superski table, and runs from 4 December 2026 to 4 April 2027.
