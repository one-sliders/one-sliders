Val Gardena city overview

Val Gardena runs up from Ortisei at 1,230 metres past Santa Cristina to Selva under the Sella, and it is the largest single area in the Dolomiti Superski table: 79 lifts and 129 slopes, open 4 December 2026 to 4 April 2027.
