Madonna di Campiglio city overview

Madonna di Campiglio sits at 1,522 metres between the Brenta Dolomites and the Adamello glaciers, and its lifts do not stop at the village: 58 of them link Campiglio with Folgarida Marilleva and Pinzolo, joining Val di Sole to Val Rendena. Together they hold 155 kilometres of slopes across 104 runs, with four snowparks and a sled run.
