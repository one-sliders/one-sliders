Bormio city overview

Bormio sits at 1,225 metres where three valleys meet, and the mountain above it does something few Alpine resorts can: one uninterrupted run of 8.5 kilometres from Cima Bianca at 3,017 metres down to the town, dropping 1,817 vertical metres - the greatest skiable vertical in Italy by the operator's own account. The Stelvio, the World Cup downhill, starts at 2,255 metres on the same slope.
