Kronplatz city overview

Kronplatz - Plan de Corones in Italian - is a single broad dome between Bruneck, St. Vigil and Olang, and the operator describes it as a gently sloping mountain with a 360-degree panorama from the top. It carries 31 lifts and 82 slopes, runs from 28 November 2026 to 11 April 2027, and sits on the Dolomiti Superski pass with its 1,200 kilometres.

Hotels & areas

Book against high season. Bruneck is the town with most to do in the evening; Olang and St. Vigil are closer to the snow.

Stay overview

Three towns ring the mountain and each has its own gondola: Bruneck on the north side with the shops and the railway, Olang to the east, St. Vigil in its own side valley to the south.
