Livigno city overview

Livigno lies at 1,816 metres in a high valley near the Swiss border, with two mountains facing each other across the town: Mottolino on one side, Carosello 3000 on the other. Between them the operator counts 115 kilometres of slopes, served by 6 gondolas, 14 chairlifts and 12 ski lifts, with the ski area opening on Saturday 28 November.
