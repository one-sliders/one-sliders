Cervinia city overview

Breuil-Cervinia sits at just over 2,000 metres on the Italian side of the Matterhorn, and its lift network reaches across the border: the operator counts Breuil-Cervinia, Valtournenche, Chamois, Torgnon and Zermatt as one ski area. Snowpark Cervinia has run at the foot of the mountain since 2003, and the Plateau Rosa glacier lifts keep turning outside the winter season.
