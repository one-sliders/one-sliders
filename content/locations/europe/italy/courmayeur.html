Courmayeur city overview

Courmayeur sits at 1,224 metres on the Italian side of Mont Blanc, which at 4,810 metres is the highest peak in the Alps, in Italy and in Europe. The cable car from the village opens a ski area the operator counts as 100 kilometres of groomed slopes and off-piste terrain across 33 runs, with separate freeride, ski-touring and heliski terrain beyond it.
