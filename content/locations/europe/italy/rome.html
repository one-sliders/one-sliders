Rome city overview

Rome works best as a base in Italy when you anchor days around Colosseum, Roman Forum and Pantheon. Use those sights to understand the city's history, street life and visitor rhythm. Rome Fiumicino handles the main arrival flow, so hotel choice should balance the centre, transfer time and day-trip plans.

Start with Centro Storico for the easiest first Rome stay, then compare Monti and Trastevere when price, nightlife, quieter evenings or transfer timing matter more than being beside every major sight.
