Sestriere city overview

Sestriere sits on its pass at 2,035 metres - the operator claims it as the highest resort in Europe - with 20 lifts and 85 slopes of its own. It is the busiest of the eight resorts on the Vialattea pass, which together open 400 kilometres of skiable area running west across the border to Montgenèvre in France.

Stay overview
