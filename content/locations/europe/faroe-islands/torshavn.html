Tórshavn city overview

Torshavn is one of the world's smallest capitals, a harbour town of turf roofs, ferries and weather-shaped island life. Start at Tinganes, visit Torshavn Cathedral and the Nordic House, then use the harbour lanes as a base for trips to Kirkjubour or northern viewpoints. Vagar Airport is the Faroe Islands' flight gateway.

1271In 1271, a royal trade monopoly was established in Tórshavn by the Norwegian Crown.

Stay overview

Stay by Torshavn harbour for restaurants and ferry atmosphere, Tinganes for old-town character, Hoyvik for apartments and drivers, or Argir for quieter views and easier southern road access.

Hotels & areas

Torshavn is compact, but weather and island routes make logistics important. Harbour stays are easiest without a car, while Hoyvik and Argir suit travellers driving to villages, tunnels and ferry connections.
