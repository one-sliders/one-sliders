Rovaniemi city overview

Rovaniemi is Lapland's Arctic Circle capital, built around winter travel, river landscapes and Santa Claus Village. Start at Santa Claus Village, visit Arktikum for northern history, then use Ounasvaara or Kemijoki riverside for outdoor time. Rovaniemi Airport is close, but winter arrivals need realistic transfer and clothing plans.

Stay overview

Stay in Rovaniemi centre for restaurants and tour pickups, Santa Claus Village for family Christmas travel, Ounasvaara for nature and winter sports, or Korkalovaara for apartments and car-based stays.

Hotels & areas

Rovaniemi is very seasonal. Centre hotels are most practical, Santa Claus Village is themed and family-focused, Ounasvaara is calmer and outdoorsy, and apartments work well when tours include pickups.

The centre is best for restaurants, shopping, tour pickups, the station and easy riverside walks.
