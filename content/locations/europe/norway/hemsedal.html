Hemsedal city overview

Hemsedal has the largest vertical in this batch at 810 metres, spread over three peaks. From Totten at 1,450 metres you can ski six kilometres without stopping, 31 of the 47 slopes are graded very easy or easy, and the mountain takes 326 centimetres of snow a year.

Families

  • 31 of the 47 slopes are graded very easy or easy, and 5 of the 22 lifts are conveyor belts for beginners.
  • Children's areas and a ski school are on site.

Strong skiers

  • 810 metres of vertical, the largest in this batch, and 6 km of uninterrupted skiing from Totten 2 at 1,450 m.
  • Off-piste skiing runs right next to the groomed slopes.
