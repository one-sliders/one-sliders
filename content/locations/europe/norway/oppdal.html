Oppdal city overview

Oppdal sits between Trollheimen and Dovrefjell with four ski areas on one pass: Vangslia, Stølen, Hovden and Ådalen. Fifteen lifts serve 30 runs, and the village is a stop on the Oslo-Trondheim railway.

Stay overview

Four areas on one mountainside means a group can split up in the morning and still meet for lunch. Vangslia is closest to the village; Hovden and Ådalen are the quiet ends.
