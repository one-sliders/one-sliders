Narvik city overview

Narvik works best as a base in Norway when you anchor days around Narvikfjellet, Ofoten Railway and Narvik War Museum. Use those sights to understand the city's history, street life and visitor rhythm. Narvik International Airport handles the main arrival flow, so hotel choice should balance the centre, transfer time and day-trip plans.
