Kvitfjell city overview

Kvitfjell has the steepest drop of the ten Norwegian resorts in this batch: 854 metres over 34 kilometres of alpine runs, served by 14 lifts. The mountain hosts Alpine World Cup finals and the world's best train on the same slopes, and it is three hours from Oslo.

Stay overview

Kvitfjell is skied from two sides. The west side is the original one with the downhill course; the east side is newer and holds the family areas. Ringebu, with its stave church, is the village in the valley below.
