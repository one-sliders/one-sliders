Hafjell city overview

Hafjell rises above Øyer with 18 lifts and 67 runs over 835 height metres, fifteen kilometres up the valley from Lillehammer. The mountain held the alpine events of the 1994 Winter Olympics and has been a Norwegian World Cup venue since.

Stay overview

Hafjell is a lift base rather than a village. The beds at the foot of the slopes are the ski-in ones; Lillehammer, fifteen kilometres away, has the rest and the restaurants.
