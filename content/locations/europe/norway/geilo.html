Geilo city overview

Geilo sits at 800 metres on the Oslo-Bergen railway, which makes it one of the few Norwegian ski villages you can reach without a car. The season runs from November to April, the slopes are floodlit on Tuesdays and Fridays, and the cross-country tracks at the stadium open in late October.
