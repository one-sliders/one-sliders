Stavanger city overview

Stavanger is a Norway base best planned around named sights rather than a generic stopover. Start with Gamle Stavanger, add Norwegian Petroleum Museum, and use Lysefjord to understand the city's history, street life and visitor rhythm. Stavanger Sola handles the main arrival flow, so hotel choice should balance the centre, transfer time and day-trip plans.
