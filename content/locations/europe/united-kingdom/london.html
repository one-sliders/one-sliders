London city overview

London is a United Kingdom base best planned around named sights rather than a generic stopover. Start with Tower of London, add British Museum, and use Buckingham Palace to understand the city's history, street life and visitor rhythm. London Heathrow handles the main arrival flow, so hotel choice should balance the centre, transfer time and day-trip plans.
