London city overview

London works best as a base in United Kingdom when you anchor days around Tower of London, British Museum and Buckingham Palace. Use those sights to understand the city's history, street life and visitor rhythm. London Heathrow handles the main arrival flow, so hotel choice should balance the centre, transfer time and day-trip plans.

Flights & airports

London Heathrow Airport (LHR) arrival plan

  • London Heathrow Airport (LHR)About 24 km from the centre

    To the centre: Heathrow Express to Paddington (every 15 min) or Piccadilly line Tube, about 18 min (Oyster/contactless on the Tube; Express ticket separate).

  • London Gatwick Airport (LGW)About 40 km from the centre

    To the centre: Gatwick Express to Victoria (every 15 min), about 30 min (train ticket or contactless).

  • London Luton Airport (LTN)About 44 km from the centre

    To the centre: Shuttle bus to Luton Airport Parkway, then train to central London, about 45 min (train ticket).

Public transport
