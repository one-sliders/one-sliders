Saalbach-Hinterglemm city overview

Saalbach Hinterglemm rings the Glemm valley with 270 kilometres of pistes across Salzburg and Tyrol, and 140 of those kilometres are graded blue - more than half the ski area. A winding 3.2-kilometre run leads down to the centre of Hinterglemm.
