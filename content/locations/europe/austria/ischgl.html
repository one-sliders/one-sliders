Ischgl city overview

Ischgl runs 239 kilometres of slopes and snow parks up to 2,872 metres, and its 2026/27 season is published in full: 26 November 2026 to 2 May 2027, one of the longest in the Alps. The ski area crosses the border into Samnaun in Switzerland.

Stay overview

Ischgl itself is where the Silvretta gondola and the nightlife are; Galtür further up the Paznaun is quieter and cheaper; Samnaun is the duty-free Swiss end of the same ski area.
