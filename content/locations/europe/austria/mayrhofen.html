Mayrhofen city overview

Mayrhofen sits at 633 metres in the Zillertal, with 142 kilometres of pistes and 61 lifts above it across Ahorn, Penken, Finkenberg, Rastkogel and Eggalm. The Penken side holds the Harakiri, which the operator calls Austria's steepest slope experience at a 78% gradient, and the PenkenPark; Ahorn is the wide, sunny side, with a 5.5 kilometre black valley run dropping more than 1,300 metres back to the village.

Book against high season. Staying in Mayrhofen means walking to a gondola; the neighbouring villages need the ski bus.

Stay overview

The village is the base for both mountains: the Penkenbahn and the Ahornbahn leave from Mayrhofen itself, and a free ski bus runs to the Horberg and Mosl lifts. Finkenberg and Hippach are quieter and cheaper, on the same lift network.
