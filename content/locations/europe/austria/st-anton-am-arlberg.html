St. Anton am Arlberg city overview

St. Anton sits at 1,304 metres on the Ski Arlberg network, which the operator calls Austria's largest ski resort: 301 kilometres of slopes and 85 lifts, reaching 2,811 metres at Valluga's peak. More than 200 kilometres of the terrain is marked as powder descent rather than piste, and nine metres of natural snow falls in an average season.
