Obergurgl city overview

Obergurgl sits at the closed head of the Ötztal at just over 2,000 metres, and the ski area above it runs from 1,800 to 3,030 metres - high enough that the operator states a hundred per cent snow guarantee and skiing from November to April.

Stay overview

Two villages, both on the slopes: Obergurgl at 1,930 metres is the larger one with the village centre, and Hochgurgl at 2,150 metres is smaller, higher and quieter. The Top Express links them in nine minutes.
