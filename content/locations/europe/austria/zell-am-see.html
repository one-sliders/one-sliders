Zell am See city overview

Zell am See sits on the shore of its lake at 764 metres, with the Schmittenhöhe rising straight out of the town. The mountain holds 77 kilometres of pistes - 30 blue, 28 red and 19 black - served by 27 lift facilities, six of them express lifts that feed the area from Schüttdorf, Zell am See and Viehhofen. Five valley runs each drop more than a thousand vertical metres back to the lake.

Families

  • 30 of the 77 piste kilometres are graded easy, and the children’s areas are built around Schmidolin, the Schmitten dragon.

Strong skiers

  • 19 km of black runs, eight marked ski routes for off-piste, and five valley runs with more than 1,000 vertical metres each.

Park riders

  • The Snowpark at 1,715 m splits into a beginner and an advanced area, and the Funslope XXL runs over a kilometre from 1,765 m.

Book against high season. The town puts you by the lake and within reach of two lifts; Schüttdorf is cheaper and has its own gondola.

Stay overview

The town is on the lake, with the Schmittenhöhebahn and the cityXpress leaving from its edge. Schüttdorf is the quieter end, served by the areitXpress, and Viehhofen is over the shoulder of the mountain on the zellamseeXpress.
