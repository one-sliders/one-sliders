Sölden city overview

Sölden has 147.2 kilometres of slopes served by 31 lifts, and three peaks above 3,000 metres that the resort calls the Big 3. Two glacier roads climb out of the Ötztal, and the Gaislachkogl summit station stands on bare rock.
