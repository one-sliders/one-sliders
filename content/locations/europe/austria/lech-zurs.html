Lech Zürs city overview

Lech lies at 1,442 metres on the Vorarlberg side of the Arlberg, on the same 301-kilometre network as St. Anton. The Rufikopf cable car leaves from the village centre, Zürs sits higher towards the Flexen pass, and Oberlech above the village is closed to cars and reached by cable car.
