Kitzbühel city overview

Kitzbühel is a medieval walled town with a World Cup downhill above it. KitzSki links seven municipalities across Tyrol and Salzburg with 233 kilometres of piste between 800 and 2,000 metres, ski racing has been held here since 1895, and the 2026/27 season runs from 6 November 2026 to 4 April 2027.

Travel tips

Free for beginners

Every village in the ski area has a practice lift in the valley that is free of charge - ten in all.

When it opens

Resterkogel opens 6 November 2026, the Hahnenkamm core area in late November, and the full connection from 11 December 2026 to 29 March 2027.

Groomed or not

Of the 233 piste kilometres, 196 are groomed; a further 41 km is marked as ski route and left ungroomed.

Parking

KitzSki shows live capacity for the car parks at each valley station, and the Fleckalmbahn, Pengelstein, Maierl and Gaisberg car parks are free.
