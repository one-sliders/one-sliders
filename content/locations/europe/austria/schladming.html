Schladming city overview

Schladming sits at 738 metres in the Styrian Ennstal, under four mountains linked into one ski area: Planai above the town, Hochwurzen, Hauser Kaibling and Reiteralm. Together they hold 123 kilometres of pistes, 50 lifts and 56 mountain huts. The Hochwurzen adds floodlit night skiing and a seven kilometre toboggan run, the longest in the region.

Stay overview

The town sits at the foot of the Planai, with the cable car leaving from the edge of the centre. Rohrmoos and Pichl are spread along the valley towards Hochwurzen and Reiteralm, and Haus im Ennstal sits under Hauser Kaibling.
