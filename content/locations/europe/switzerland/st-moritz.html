Hotels & areas

Book against high season. Dorf puts you by the funicular, Bad by the lake, Celerina on the quiet side of Corviglia.

Stay overview

St. Moritz Dorf sits above the lake with the Chantarella funicular to Corviglia; St. Moritz Bad is by the water and cheaper. Celerina lies at the foot of the same mountain on the other side.
