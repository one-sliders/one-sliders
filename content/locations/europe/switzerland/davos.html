Davos city overview

Davos sits at 1,560 metres, the highest town in the Alps, and it does not have one ski mountain but six: Parsenn, Jakobshorn, Schatzalp-Strela, Madrisa, Pischa and Rinerhorn. Together they hold 300 kilometres of snow-sure slopes served by 57 transport systems. Parsenn alone has around 97 kilometres; the Jakobshorn has 55 from blue to black, and carries the JatzPark at around 2,500 metres.

Stay overview

Davos Platz and Davos Dorf sit at either end of the same street, each with its own railway station and its own lift: the Parsennbahn from Dorf, the Jakobshorn cable car from Platz. Klosters is over the Parsenn on the other side.
