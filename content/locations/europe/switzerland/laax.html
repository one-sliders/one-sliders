Laax city overview

Laax sits in the Surselva valley in Grisons, and what the resort publishes about itself is almost entirely freestyle: five snow parks holding over 90 obstacles, a Pro Kicker Line of four Olympic-sized kickers strung over a full kilometre of piste, and a superpipe in Snowpark NoName that runs 220 metres long, 22 metres wide and 7 metres high - which the operator calls the largest in Europe.
