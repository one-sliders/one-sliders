Crans-Montana city overview

Crans-Montana sits on a terrace at 1,547 metres above the Rhone valley, and its ski area runs from the Plaine Morte Glacier at 3,000 metres down to the golf course on the Haut-Plateau at 1,500. The operator describes all 140 kilometres of marked pistes as completely south-facing - the whole mountain turned towards the sun and the Valais Alps opposite.
