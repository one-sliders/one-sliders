Verbier city overview

Verbier has 106 kilometres of runs and 33 lifts of its own, on a 4 Vallées network that runs to 410 kilometres - the largest ski area entirely within Switzerland. Mont-Fort tops the network at 3,330 metres, and the speed-skiing run drops 383 metres over 1,400.
