Arosa Lenzerheide city overview

Two resorts, one ski area: Arosa at the head of its own valley and Lenzerheide on the pass, joined since 2013 by the Urdenbahn cable car across the ridge between them. The operator's live panel counts 43 lifts, 225 kilometres of slopes, eight snowpark areas, twelve toboggan runs and 57 mountain restaurants. The page is placed on Lenzerheide, the side most visitors stay on.

Stay overview

Lenzerheide lies along the pass road with the Rothorn lifts above it; Arosa is at the end of its own railway in the next valley. The Urdenbahn links the two halves of the mountain between them.
