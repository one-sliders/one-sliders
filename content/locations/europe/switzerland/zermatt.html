Zermatt city overview

Zermatt is closed to combustion cars and reached by train from Täsch, and its ski area climbs to 3,883 metres at Matterhorn Glacier Paradise - the highest lift-served point of the eighty resorts in this project. The village itself sits at 1,608 metres under the Matterhorn.

Stay overview

The village is car-free, so everything is walked or taken by electric taxi. Winkelmatten at the southern end is quieter and closer to the Matterhorn lifts; Täsch down the valley is where the road and the car parks end.
