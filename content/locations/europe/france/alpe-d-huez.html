Alpe d'Huez city overview

Alpe d'Huez sits on a sunny plateau at 1,827 metres with more than 250 kilometres of slopes above it. The Sarenne drops 16 kilometres without a break, which the resort calls the longest black slope in the world, and the old village of Huez lies down the hairpins below.

Families

  • 34 green and 31 blue of the 111 marked pistes - more than half the runs are graded for beginners or easy skiing.

Strong skiers

  • 2,000 metres of vertical from the Pic Blanc at 3,330 m, and 16 black runs including La Sarenne.

Park riders

  • Five snowparks, with a 900 m expert line of 12 to 25 m tables.
