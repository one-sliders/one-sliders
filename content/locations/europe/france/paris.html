Paris city overview

Paris is a dense capital of river walks, museums, neighbourhood cafes and landmark views. Start with the Eiffel Tower and the Seine, give the Louvre Museum a focused visit, then use Le Marais, Saint-Germain or Montmartre for a slower neighbourhood day. Charles de Gaulle and Orly airports both connect by rail and road.

Worth seeing

Eiffel Tower

The Eiffel Tower is Paris's most recognisable landmark, best planned with timed tickets or viewpoints from across the Seine.

Notre-Dame Cathedral

Notre-Dame Cathedral anchors the Île de la Cité and remains central to Paris river walks and Gothic history.

Louvre Museum

The Louvre Museum is vast, so a focused visit around selected galleries works better than rushing every wing.

Champs-Élysées

Champs-Élysées is a named stop in Paris worth keeping on the route when it fits the day's transport, timing and neighborhood plan.

Montmartre

Montmartre gives Paris a strong culture stop for museums, exhibitions or local collections, and works best as an indoor anchor in the day's route.

Seine riverbanks

Seine riverbanks adds the water-facing side of Paris, making it a good break from museums, traffic and dense historic streets.

Stay overview

Le Marais is a strong first base, Saint-Germain is polished and walkable, the Latin Quarter is practical, Opéra suits shopping and transport, and Montmartre works for atmosphere if hills are acceptable.

Hotels & areas

Paris hotel choice should follow metro lines and neighbourhood mood. Stay central for short trips, choose Opéra for transport convenience, or pick Montmartre and the Left Bank when atmosphere matters more than speed.

Travel tips

Best base

Choose Le Marais or Saint-Germain for a first visit if you want cafes, river walks and museums close together.

Museum timing

Book Louvre and Eiffel Tower tickets ahead, then keep museum plans focused rather than trying to see everything.

Airport routes

Check whether CDG or Orly is better for your hotel, because cross-city transfers can be slow.

Day trip

Keep one side-trip day flexible from Paris, then choose it only after checking transport frequency and opening hours.
