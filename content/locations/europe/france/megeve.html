Megève city overview

Megève is a village first and a ski resort second, a settlement at 1,105 metres rather than a complex built for the lifts. Its skiing sits on the Evasion Mont-Blanc pass, over 400 kilometres of linked slopes across the valley, with the lift operator's own Megève sector reaching 2,350 metres and the resort's accessible areas at Jaillet and Combloux.
