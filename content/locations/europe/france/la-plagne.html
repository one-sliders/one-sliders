La Plagne city overview

La Plagne spreads across a mountainside at around 2,000 metres with 225 kilometres of pistes beneath and above it, running from 1,250 metres up to a glacier at 3,080 - a crossing of every Alpine layer, as the operator puts it, from dolines and forest to ice. Fifty-two of its runs are graded red or black, and the season runs 12 December 2026 to 24 April 2027.
