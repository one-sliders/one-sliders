Val Thorens city overview

Val Thorens sits at 2,300 metres, the highest resort in Les 3 Vallées, on a network of 600 kilometres spanning three valleys and seven resorts. The altitude is the point: it is where the season starts earliest and holds longest in the Tarentaise.

Stay overview

Val Thorens is the high end of the Belleville valley and largely car-free in its centre. Les Menuires below is cheaper on the same pass, and Méribel is the middle of the three valleys.
