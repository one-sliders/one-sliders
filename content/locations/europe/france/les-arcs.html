Les Arcs city overview

Les Arcs is four high-altitude sites above Bourg-Saint-Maurice, with 200 kilometres of slopes of its own reaching 3,226 metres and seventy per cent of them above 2,000. The Vanoise Express then crosses to La Plagne on 1,824 metres of cable without a single pylon, 380 metres above the valley floor, and the two together make Paradiski's 425 kilometres.
