Courchevel city overview

Courchevel is not one village but six, stacked up the mountainside from Saint Bon at 1,100 metres to Courchevel itself at 1,850. All of them open onto Les 3 Vallées, which the operator calls the largest ski area in the world: 600 kilometres of slopes across 337 runs and seven resorts, with 85 per cent of it above 1,800 metres. The season runs 4 December to 18 April.

Stay overview

The six villages each have their own character and their own price: Le Praz and La Tania are quiet and wooded, Village and Moriond are family ground, and Courchevel 1850 is the one the resort was built around.
