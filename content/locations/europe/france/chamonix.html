Chamonix city overview

Chamonix is not one linked mountain but a valley of separate sectors, each with its own cable car: Brévent at 2,525 metres, Flégère at 2,396, Balme at 2,270 above Le Tour and Vallorcine, and Grands Montets at 2,765 above Argentière. Over all of them the Aiguille du Midi climbs to 3,842 metres, the starting point for the off-piste descents the valley is known for.
