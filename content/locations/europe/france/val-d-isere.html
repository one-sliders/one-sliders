Val d'Isère city overview

Val d'Isère sits at 1,849 metres at the head of the Tarentaise, and the skiing above it runs to over 3,400: 71 lifts and 300 kilometres of marked runs, split by the operator into 23 green, 67 blue, 41 red and 29 black.

Hotels & areas

Book against high season. The village centre puts you between both main lifts; La Daille and Le Fornet are cheaper.

Stay overview

The main village sits along one road with the Bellevarde and Solaise lifts at either end; La Daille is the gateway hamlet at the bottom, and Le Fornet is the quiet old village up the valley.
