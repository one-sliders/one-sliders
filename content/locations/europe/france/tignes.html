Tignes city overview

Tignes shares its 300 kilometres of pistes with Val d'Isère across the ridge, and what it keeps for itself is the Grande Motte: a cable car to 3,456 metres on the glacier, and a descent from there to Val Claret that drops 1,400 vertical metres with your skis on the whole way. The area runs down to 1,550 metres at Les Brévières, and opens progressively from 21 November 2026.

Stay overview

Tignes is several villages at different heights: Val Claret highest at the foot of the Grande Motte, Tignes le Lac by the water, and Les Brévières and Les Boisses down at the dam.
