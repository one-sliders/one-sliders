Worth seeing

  • Barcelona Gaudi: Sagrada Familia, Park Guell and Casa Batllo define Gaudi's visionary Barcelona.
  • Madrid Prado: One of the world's great art museums, with Velazquez, Goya and El Greco.
  • Bilbao Guggenheim: Frank Gehry's titanium museum sits on the Nervion river and anchors Bilbao's modern city image.
  • Alhambra Granada: A Moorish palace complex of intricate tilework and water gardens.
