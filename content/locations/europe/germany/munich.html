Munich city overview

Munich is Bavaria's polished capital, balancing beer halls, art museums, royal squares and Alpine access. Start at Marienplatz, visit the Residenz or English Garden, then plan Oktoberfest around Theresienwiese if your dates match. Munich Airport is northeast of the city, with S-Bahn and airport buses into town.

Stay overview

Altstadt is easiest for a first visit, Maxvorstadt works for museums, Ludwigsvorstadt is practical for the station and Oktoberfest, and Schwabing suits restaurants, nightlife and English Garden access.

Hotels & areas

Munich hotels get expensive during Oktoberfest and trade fairs. Book early, check U-Bahn access, and choose Ludwigsvorstadt only when station or Theresienwiese convenience outweighs atmosphere.
