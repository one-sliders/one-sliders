Branäs city overview

Branäs rises above the Klarälven valley in Värmland with 32 lifts, 36 slopes and 415 metres of vertical - more drop than Sälen. It is the nearest large Swedish resort to both Oslo and Gothenburg, and the mountain carries a Fun Park, a Funslope and a Nintendo Land for children.
