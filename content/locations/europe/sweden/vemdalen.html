Vemdalen city overview

Vemdalen is three ski areas on one SkiPass in Jämtland: Vemdalsskalet between two mountains, Björnrike with its own village, and Klövsjö with Storhogna. Together they hold 44 lifts and 62 slopes, the vertical runs to 470 metres, and over 155 kilometres of cross-country track connect them.

Stay overview

The three areas are separate villages, not three sides of one hill. Vemdalsskalet is the liveliest and has the fast lifts; Björnrike is quieter and gentler; Klövsjö and Storhogna share the greenest terrain of the three.
