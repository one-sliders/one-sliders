Sälen city overview

Sälen is four ski areas on one SkiPass, and together they hold 105 lifts and 101 slopes - more of both than anywhere else in Sweden. Lindvallen and Högfjället are where beginners go, Tandådalen has the Big Air Arena and the most difficult slopes, and families have been coming here since 1975. The Vasaloppet starts in the valley below.

What the mountain suits

Facts as the operator publishes them, not a score. Read the numbers and decide.

Families

  • Högfjället is entirely green and blue: 7 very easy slopes and 2 easy, and nothing harder.
  • Lindvallen has 24 of its 42 slopes graded very easy or easy, plus a children's area and an adventure pool with waves and a FlowRider.
  • Families have skied here since 1975, and Trollskogen lets children ski through a troll forest.

Strong skiers

  • Tandådalen has 7 difficult slopes, the most of any area in Sälen, from wide carving runs to steeper skiing.
  • The Big Air Arena is at Tandådalen.

Stay overview

The four areas are far enough apart that the one you book decides your week. Lindvallen has the adventure pool and the evening skiing; Hundfjället has ski-in ski-out at SkiStar Lodge and the troll forest; Högfjället is the hotel, the afterski and nine green and blue slopes; Tandådalen is the snowpark and the steep end.

Travel tips

Which area

Högfjället is entirely green and blue, Tandådalen has seven difficult slopes. Pick the base that matches who is skiing.

Evening skiing

Magic Mountain runs every day at Valleberget in Lindvallen, with a light show.

Two resorts on one pass

A SkiPass of three days or more in Sälen includes two days in Trysil across the Norwegian border.

Train from 2026/27

Snälltåget runs to Mora with a transfer bus onward to Sälen.
