Örebro city overview

Örebro works best as a base in Sweden when you anchor days around Orebro Castle, Wadkoping and Stadsparken. Use those sights to understand the city's history, street life and visitor rhythm. Örebro International Airport handles the main arrival flow, so hotel choice should balance the centre, transfer time and day-trip plans.

City history

1404The prefix Öre- is derived from ör 'gravel (bank)'. Örebro received its Royal Charter and city privileges not later than 1404.
1810Notable events in Örebro's history include the national diet meeting at Örebro in 1810, where Jean-Baptiste Bernadotte was elected crown prince of Sweden.
19th centuryAlthough a trade town, Örebro remained small until the second half of the 19th century, when it grew rapidly as a center of the national shoe-manufacturing industry. 2025 Risbergska school shooting On 4 February.
13th centuryThe location became a natural seat of commerce in the Scandinavian Middle Ages and is mentioned in print in the 13th century.
Civic roleThe natural center of the city is otherwise Örebro Castle, situated on an islet in the Svartån, and dividing the town into a northern and a southern part.
Regional roleAccording to Swedish prime minister Ulf Kristersson, it is the deadliest mass shooting in the country's history.
