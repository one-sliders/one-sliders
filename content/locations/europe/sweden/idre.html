Idre city overview

Idre Fjäll is Sweden's southernmost mountain, and its 41 slopes face in every direction, so there is usually shelter whichever way the wind blows. Almost all the accommodation is ski-in ski-out with the shops and restaurants on foot, and the 2026/27 season runs from 19 December to 4 April under a snow guarantee.

Stay overview

Idre Fjäll is one resort with fourteen named cabin areas rather than several villages. Almost all of them are ski-in ski-out, so the question is how far you want to walk to the grocery store and the restaurants, not how far to the lift.
