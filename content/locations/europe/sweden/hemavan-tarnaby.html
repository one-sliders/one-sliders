Hemavan city overview

Hemavan sits on the edge of the Vindelfjällen nature reserve near the Norwegian border, with ten lifts including a gondola, 38 runs and three separate children's areas. Sixty-five kilometres of cross-country track run from the village, some of it floodlit, and Tärnaby lies eighteen kilometres down the valley.

Stay overview

Hemavan has the gondola and the children's areas; Tärnaby, eighteen kilometres down the valley, has the Fjällhotell and the Sporthotell and is the quieter and cheaper of the two.
