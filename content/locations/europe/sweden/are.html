Åre city overview

Åre is the largest ski resort in Sweden, and one SkiPass covers three separate areas: Åre by at the foot of Åreskutan, Åre Björnen laid out for children and beginners, and Duved/Tegefjäll eight kilometres down the valley. The vertical is 890 metres, the most in the country, and the season in Åre by opens on 11 December 2026.

What the mountain suits

Facts as the operator publishes them, not a score. Read the numbers and decide.

Families

  • Åre Björnen is laid out for children and beginners: 16 of its 18 slopes are graded very easy or easy.
  • Ski school and a children's area with Valle activities are on site.
  • Duved/Tegefjäll has a children's area and shorter lift queues than Åre by.

Strong skiers

  • Åre högzon runs above the treeline, where 8 of 15 slopes are graded intermediate and none is easier than very easy.
  • 890 metres of vertical, the largest in Sweden.
  • Voted Sweden's best off-piste mountain in 2023/24 and again in 2024/25.

Stay overview

Where you sleep in Åre decides which of the three ski areas you ski first. Åre by puts the funicular and the Kabinbanan within walking distance; Åre Björnen puts accommodation beside the beginner slopes; Duved and Tegefjäll trade the village for shorter lift queues and their own cheaper area pass.

Travel tips

Which area

Åre Björnen has 16 of its 18 slopes graded very easy or easy; Åre högzon has 8 of 15 graded intermediate and nothing below very easy.

Opening dates

Åre by opens 11 December 2026 and Bergbanan runs to 22:00 from 18 December. The Kabinbanan opens 8 February 2027.

One pass, three areas

The Åre SkiPass covers Åre by, Åre Björnen, Duved and Tegefjäll; a cheaper Area Pass covers Duved and Tegefjäll only.

Ski bus

The ski bus between accommodation and the ski areas is included with the SkiPass.
