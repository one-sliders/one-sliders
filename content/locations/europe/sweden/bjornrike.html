Björnrike city overview

Björnrike is one of Vemdalen's three ski areas and has its own village at the foot of the slopes. Fifteen lifts serve sixteen slopes, three of the lifts are conveyor belts for beginners, and the same SkiPass reaches Vemdalsskalet and Klövsjö with Storhogna.
