Funäsdalen city overview

Funäsdalen sits in the middle of Funäsfjällen, six separate ski areas holding more than 153 runs between them. Tänndalen has the most pistes at 57, Ramundberget the most descents at 50, and 300 kilometres of connecting cross-country track runs between the villages.

Stay overview

Funäsfjällen is six villages rather than one resort. Funäsdalen itself has the cable car up Funäsdalsberget and the most services; Ramundberget and Tänndalen have the most skiing; Tännäskröket and Tänndalsvallen are small and quiet.

Travel tips

Which area

Tänndalen has 57 pistes and 18 lifts; Tänndalsvallen has 2 pistes and 1 lift. They are not interchangeable.

Cross-country first

300 kilometres of connecting track links five of the six areas, and Kappruet has 100 km of its own.

Off-piste at Tännäskröket

Tännäskröket marks 8 runs as off-piste alongside its 9 groomed descents.

Pre-season

Bruksvallarna opens for cross-country from 24 October.
