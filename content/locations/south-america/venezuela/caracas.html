Caracas city overview

Caracas is Venezuela's mountain-framed capital, set below Avila National Park with grand civic spaces, museums and sharp contrasts between neighborhoods. Use Chacao or Altamira as a practical base, visit Plaza Bolivar and the Museo de Bellas Artes by day, then plan Waraira Repano or Galipan with local advice. Maiquetia airport is on the coast, so transfers require care and timing.

Travel tips

Safety first

Use current local advice, arranged transfers and trusted taxis; avoid displaying phones, jewelry or cameras in public.

Best base

Choose Chacao or Altamira for a first stay because hotels, restaurants and transport logistics are more manageable.

Airport transfers

Pre-arrange Maiquetia transfers through a hotel or trusted operator, especially for late arrivals or early departures.

Mountain plans

Visit Avila or Galipan with clear weather, local guidance and enough daylight for the return journey.
