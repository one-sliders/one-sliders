Georgetown city overview

Georgetown is Guyana's low-rise coastal capital, built around canals, wooden colonial architecture and a busy market-and-seawall rhythm. Start at St George's Cathedral, walk the Stabroek Market area in daylight, then use the Seawall or Promenade Gardens for a slower break. Cheddi Jagan airport handles international arrivals well outside the city, while Ogle is useful for domestic flights.

City history

1748In 1748, Governor Laurens Storm van 's Gravesande built a guard post at the mouth of the Demerara River.
1782The town was established in 1782 during a brief occupation by the French of the Dutch colony of Demerara.
1784The original name of Longchamps was changed to Stabroek in 1784, after Nicholaas Geelvinck (1732 — 1787), Lord of Stabroek, the then President of the Dutch West India Company.
1812The city's name changed again in 1812 when, under British rule, it became Georgetown, in honour of King George III.
1842Georgetown gained official city status on 24 August 1842 during the reign of Queen Victoria.
1945In 1945, a large fire (The Great Fire) broke out in the city, causing widespread damage.

Stay overview

Use Kingston or Queenstown for the simplest Georgetown stay, especially for business, embassies or guesthouses. Stabroek is central but hectic, Bourda is practical for food and cricket, and Kitty helps with seawall access and coastal movement.

Hotels & areas

Georgetown hotel choice is about transfers and comfort rather than walking everywhere: Kingston is the safest default, Queenstown is quieter, Stabroek is central by day, Bourda feels local, and Kitty works for seawall-side plans.

Travel tips

Best base

Choose Kingston or Queenstown for a first stay because they balance hotel comfort, taxis and access to central Georgetown.

Airport transfers

Pre-book Cheddi Jagan transfers, especially for late arrivals, because the airport is well outside the city.

Safety

Visit markets and downtown streets in daylight, use taxis at night and keep phones and valuables discreet.

Interior trips

Kaieteur Falls and rainforest lodges usually require flights or specialist operators, not casual same-day road planning.
