Lome city overview

Lome is Togo's breezy Atlantic capital, hugging the coast right up to the Ghanaian border. First-time visitors should browse fabrics and beads at the Grand Marché, learn about traditional medicine at the Akodessewa Fetish Market, and admire the twin-spired Lomé Cathedral downtown. Lomé–Tokoin International Airport sits inside the city, and Lome's beaches and West African crafts scene make it an easy, walkable capital to explore.

Stay overview

Base in Centre-ville for an easy first stay near the Grand Marché, cathedral and government offices, or choose Kodjoviakopé for beachfront hotels and nightlife near the border. Tokoin suits longer, more local stays, while Bè rewards a guided visit for its traditional culture.

Travel tips

Best base

Choose Centre-ville for markets and sightseeing, or Kodjoviakopé for beachfront hotels and nightlife near the border.

Fetish market etiquette

Hire a local guide for the Akodessewa Fetish Market, since context helps make sense of the traditional remedies on display.

Airport convenience

Take advantage of Lomé–Tokoin International Airport's in-city location, keeping transfer times short even for late flights.

Border day trips

Consider a short crossing into Ghana via the nearby border post if extending a trip, but carry proper visas and documentation.
