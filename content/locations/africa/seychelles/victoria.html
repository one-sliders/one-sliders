Victoria city overview

Victoria is Seychelles' compact capital on Mahé, often noted as one of the world's smallest capital cities, mixing colonial architecture with Creole markets. First-time visitors should browse fresh spices and fish at Sir Selwyn Selwyn-Clarke Market, photograph the miniature Victoria Clock Tower, and wander the Botanical Gardens' giant tortoises. Seychelles International Airport lies a short drive south, and Victoria makes an easy base before heading to Mahé's beaches.

City history

Timeline

1756France claimed the Seychelles islands before the settlement that became Victoria developed on Mahe.
1778French colonists settled the area that later became Victoria, establishing the first town foundation on Mahe.
1841The British renamed the settlement Victoria after Queen Victoria; it had previously been known as L'Etablissement.
1903Seychelles became a separate Crown colony, making Victoria the administrative centre of the islands.
1976Seychelles became independent, and Victoria continued as the capital of the new republic.

Notable eras

TodayModern Victoria concentrates the port, government offices, markets and cultural institutions of Seychelles.

Stay overview

Base in Town Centre for an easy first stay near the market, clock tower and museums, then move to Beau Vallon for Mahé's best beach or Eden Island for marina views. Bel Ombre suits travelers wanting a quiet, beach-adjacent stay close to the capital.

Travel tips

Best base

Stay in Town Centre for a night or two of sightseeing, then move to Beau Vallon or another beach for the rest of a Mahé stay.

Sunday closures

Plan market visits and shopping for weekdays or Saturday morning, since much of Victoria closes on Sundays.

Airport transfers

Book a hotel transfer from Seychelles International Airport in advance, as the drive is short but taxis can be limited late at night.

Cash and pace

Carry Seychelles rupees for markets and small vendors, and expect a slow, relaxed pace typical of island life.
