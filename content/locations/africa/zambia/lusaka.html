Lusaka city overview

Lusaka is Zambia's plateau capital, a fast-growing commercial hub centred on the long, shop-lined Cairo Road. First-time visitors should browse crafts at Kabwata Cultural Village, learn Zambian history at the Lusaka National Museum, and pay respects at the Freedom Statue downtown. Kenneth Kaunda International Airport connects the city nationally and regionally, and Lusaka works well as a stopover before heading to Victoria Falls or South Luangwa's safari lodges.

Stay overview

Base along Cairo Road for an easy first stay near shops, banks and the National Museum, or choose Kabulonga for quieter, upscale streets and dining. Woodlands and Rhodes Park suit business travelers wanting central offices and embassies within reach.

Hotels & areas

Lusaka hotel choice depends on priorities: Cairo Road suits first-time visitors wanting central sights and shopping, Kabulonga suits longer, quieter stays with good restaurants, and Woodlands or Rhodes Park suit business and diplomatic visitors.

Travel tips

Best base

Choose the Lusaka base by repeat journeys: sights, evening food, station access and the airport transfer should all still work.

Onward travel

Use Lusaka as a stopover when connecting to Victoria Falls, South Luangwa or Lower Zambezi National Park.

Airport transfers

Book a hotel shuttle or taxi from Kenneth Kaunda International Airport for the roughly 30-minute ride into the city.

Craft shopping

Visit Kabwata Cultural Village for Zambian wood carvings and baskets at fairer prices than tourist-area shops.
