Kigali city overview

Kigali is Rwanda's hilltop capital, widely regarded as one of Africa's cleanest and safest cities. First-time visitors should reflect at the Kigali Genocide Memorial, browse the murals and bars of Nyamirambo, and photograph the glass dome of the Kigali Convention Centre lit up at night. Kigali International Airport connects the city to gorilla-trekking bases in Volcanoes National Park, making Kigali a natural first and last stop on a Rwanda trip.

City history

1950sUsumbura's population exceeded 50,000 during the 1950s and was the mandate's only European-style city, while Kigali's population remained at around 6,000 until independence in 1962. Post-independence era Kigali became the capital upon Rwandan independence.
1897Rwanda and neighbouring Burundi had been assigned to Germany by the Berlin Conference of 1884, forming part of German East Africa, and Germany established a presence in the country in 1897 with the formation.
1907The founding of Kigali is generally dated to 1907, when German administrator and explorer Richard Kandt was appointed as the first resident of Rwanda, and established the city as the headquarters.
1916Belgian forces took control of Rwanda and Burundi during World War I, with Kigali being captured by the Northern Brigade led by Colonel Philippe Molitor on 6 May 1916.
1994In April 1994 President Habyarimana was assassinated when his plane was shot down near Kigali International Airport.
2013A masterplan, adopted by the city and the government in 2013 and supported by international finance and labour, seeks to establish Kigali as a decentralised modern city by 2040.
14th centuryAccording to oral history, the Kingdom of Rwanda was founded in the 14th century on the shores of Lake Muhazi, around 40 kilometres (25 mi) east of modern Kigali.
1300sAlexis Kagame promoted an alternative theory that the city was established as a capital under Cyilima I Rugwe in either the 1300s or the 1500s.
TodayRwandan scholar Alexis Kagame, who did extensive research into the country's oral history and traditions, wrote that the name Kigali came into use after King Cyilima I Rugwe completed a conquest of the area.
Regional roleThe capital of the kingdom was at Nyanza, in the south of the country.
Regional roleKandt's house, located close to the central business district (CBD), was the first European-style house in the city, and remains in use today as the Kandt House Museum of Natural History.
Regional roleKigali's first businesses were established by Greek and Indian merchants, with assistance from Baganda and Swahili people.
Regional roleKigali remained relatively small through the remainder of the colonial era, as much of the administration took place in Ruanda-Urundi's capital Usumbura, now known as Bujumbura in Burundi.
Regional roleBuildings that were heavily damaged during the fighting have been demolished, much of the city has been rebuilt, and modern office buildings and infrastructure now exist across the city.

Stay overview

Base in Kiyovu for an easy first Kigali stay near hotels, banks and the Genocide Memorial, or choose Kimihurura for upscale dining and embassy-district calm. Nyamirambo suits travelers wanting local culture and walking tours, while Kacyiru works well for conference visitors.

Hotels & areas

Kigali hotel choice depends on purpose: Kiyovu suits first-time visitors wanting central hotels near the memorial and CBD, Kimihurura suits longer stays with upscale restaurants, and Kacyiru suits conference-goers close to the Kigali Convention Centre.

Kiyovu

Kiyovu is Kigali's central business hub, packed with hotels, banks and restaurants within easy walking distance of downtown offices.

Travel tips

Best base

Choose Kiyovu or Kimihurura for an easy first stay close to hotels, restaurants and the Kigali Genocide Memorial.

Cleanliness rules

Note that plastic bags are banned nationwide and littering is taken seriously, part of why Kigali stays remarkably clean.

Airport transfers

Arrange a hotel pickup at Kigali International Airport in advance, since it keeps arrivals simple after long connecting flights.

Gorilla trekking

Book Volcanoes National Park gorilla permits well ahead if combining Kigali with a trek, since daily permit numbers are strictly limited.
