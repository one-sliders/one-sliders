Hermanus city overview

Hermanus is a Western Cape coastal town on Walker Bay, world-famous for having some of the best land-based whale watching anywhere on Earth.

Stay overview

Base in Central Hermanus for an easy first stay near the Cliff Path, Old Harbour Museum and restaurants, or choose Voëlklip and Grotto Beach for a family-friendly beach stay. The Hemel-en-Aarde Valley suits wine-focused visits, while Sandbaai offers quieter, self-catering options.

Hotels & areas

Hermanus hotel choice depends on priorities: Central Hermanus suits first-time visitors wanting whale watching and restaurants within reach, Voëlklip and Grotto Beach suit families, and the Hemel-en-Aarde Valley suits travelers prioritising wine estates over town amenities.

Central Hermanus / Marine Drive

Central Hermanus along Marine Drive holds the Old Harbour Museum, shops and restaurants, all within walking distance of the Cliff Path.

Travel tips

Best base

Choose Central Hermanus for whale-watching access along the Cliff Path and easy walks to restaurants and the harbour.

Whale season

Visit between June and November for the best chance of spotting Southern Right Whales close to shore from the cliffs.

Getting here

Rent a car or book a shuttle for the roughly two-hour drive from Cape Town International Airport, since there is no local airport.

Wine route timing

Book Hemel-en-Aarde Valley tastings ahead on weekends, when the small boutique estates can fill up quickly.
