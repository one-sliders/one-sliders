South Africa

History

1652The Dutch East India Company established a supply station at the Cape.
1879The Anglo-Zulu War was fought, including the Zulu victory at Isandlwana.
1910The Union of South Africa was formed under British dominion.
1948The National Party came to power and codified apartheid.
1990-1994Nelson Mandela was released and the first democratic elections were held.
1996A new constitution with a bill of rights was adopted.

Worth seeing

  • Table Mountain: The flat-topped massif above Cape Town and its national park.
  • Kruger National Park: One of Africa's largest game reserves, on the Mozambique border.
  • Robben Island: The prison island where Mandela was held, a UNESCO World Heritage site.
