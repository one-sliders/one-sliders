Durban city overview

Durban is South Africa's subtropical Indian Ocean city, famous for its warm surf beaches and the country's largest Indian community outside India.

Stay overview

Base along the Golden Mile for an easy first Durban stay near the beach, uShaka Marine World and Moses Mabhida Stadium, or choose Umhlanga for calmer beaches and shopping malls. Berea and Morningside suit travelers wanting quieter, hillside stays with easy access to the centre.

Hotels & areas

Durban hotel choice depends on priorities: the Golden Mile suits first-time visitors wanting beach access and central sights, Umhlanga suits upscale beach stays near the airport, and Berea or Morningside suit business travelers and longer, quieter stays.

Golden Mile / Beachfront

The Golden Mile beachfront runs along Durban's main promenade, lined with hotels, surf schools and the uShaka Marine World aquarium.

Travel tips

Best base

Choose the Golden Mile for beach access and central sights, or Umhlanga for a calmer beach base near the airport.

Bunny chow

Try bunny chow, Durban's signature curry-filled bread loaf, at one of the city's long-running Indian restaurants.

Airport transfers

Take the Airport Shuttle bus or a metered taxi from King Shaka International Airport for the roughly 40-minute ride into town.

Beach safety

Swim at lifeguard-patrolled beaches along the Golden Mile and check flag warnings, since currents can be strong.
