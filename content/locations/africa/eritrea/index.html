Eritrea

History

c. 100-940The Aksumite kingdom traded through the Red Sea port of Adulis.
1890Italy proclaimed the colony of Eritrea.
1952The territory was federated with Ethiopia by UN resolution.
1961-1991A thirty-year armed struggle for independence was fought.
1993Independence was confirmed by referendum on 24 May.
1998-2000A border war with Ethiopia caused heavy casualties.
