Marrakech city overview

Marrakech is Morocco's red-walled imperial city, where the medina's Jemaa el-Fnaa square turns from a daytime market into a nightly spectacle of food stalls and performers. First-time visitors should look up at the Koutoubia Mosque's minaret, wander the tiled courtyards of Bahia Palace, and cool off among the cacti of Majorelle Garden. Marrakech Menara Airport sits minutes from the medina, making the city an easy first or last stop in Morocco.

Stay overview

Stay inside the Medina for a classic riad experience close to Jemaa el-Fnaa and Bahia Palace, or choose Gueliz for modern restaurants near Majorelle Garden. Hivernage and Palmeraie suit travelers wanting resort-style hotels and pools outside the old city walls.

Hotels & areas

Riads inside the Medina offer the most atmospheric stays near the souks and Koutoubia Mosque, while Gueliz and Hivernage suit visitors wanting swimming pools, modern dining and easier vehicle access than the old city allows.

Travel tips

Visit Jemaa el-Fnaa at dusk

Arrive at Jemaa el-Fnaa around sunset to see the square fill with food stalls, musicians and storytellers as the day cools.

Book Majorelle Garden ahead

Reserve a timed ticket for Majorelle Garden in advance, since the small garden regularly sells out during peak season.

Negotiate in the souks

Expect to bargain for carpets, lamps and leather goods in the Medina's souks, where first-quoted prices are rarely final.

Plan an Atlas day trip

Set aside a full day for the nearby Atlas Mountains or Ourika Valley if the itinerary allows time beyond the city.
