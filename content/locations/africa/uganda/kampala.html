Kampala city overview

Kampala is Uganda's hilly capital, spread across seven hills above the shores of Lake Victoria. First-time visitors should tour the UNESCO-listed Kasubi Tombs of the Buganda kings, browse crafts and produce at Owino Market, and photograph the lotus-shaped Bahá'í Temple. Entebbe International Airport sits about 40 kilometres south on the lakeshore, and Kampala's markets, music and traffic-filled energy make it a lively East African base.

City history

1870sHe also described the Kibuga in his 1870s dispatches to The New York Herald, thus: As we approached the capital, the highway from Usavara increased in width from 20 ft to 150 ft ...Arrived.
1862In 1862, when explorer John Speke arrived in Buganda, the Kibuga (capital) was at Bandabarogo, present-day Banda Hill, and the reigning Kabaka (King) was Mutesa I.
1875In 1875, explorer Henry Morton Stanley reported the capital as being at present-day Lubaga Hill, where he met the same Kabaka, Mutesa I.
1895In 1895, Mengo Senior School, the first school offering Western education in Kampala, was opened by the Church Missionary Society at Namirembe hill, where mostly the children of chiefs and pages of the royal.
1897Also in 1897, Kampala's first Western-style health facility, Mengo Hospital, was opened on Namirembe hill by British doctor and missionary Sir Albert Ruskin Cook.
1900This state of affairs later culminated in the signing of the Buganda Agreement (1900) that formalised British colonial rule in Buganda.
1906In 1906, the boundary of Kampala was set as a three-mile radius from the “present Nakasero Fort” at Old Kampala, and the Kampala Local Sanitary Board was designated as the authority for urban administration.
1922In 1922, Kampala's oldest university, Makerere, was founded as the Uganda Technical College at the present Makerere Hill and initially offered carpentry, building construction, mechanics, arts, education, agriculture, and medicine.
1931In 1931, the Uganda Railway line reached Kampala, connecting Kampala to Mombasa Port, thirty-five years after the commencement of its construction.
1962On 9 October, 1962, Uganda gained independence; subsequently the capital city was transferred from Entebbe to Kampala and in the same year, Kampala was granted city status.
OriginsKampala originally referred to only the present-day Old Kampala hill, on whose summit Fort Lugard was located, and the initial headquarters of the British colonial authorities in the soon to be Uganda Protectorate.
Regional roleHence the name "Kampala" came to refer to this initial British colonial settlement that would later on spread out from the occupied Old Kampala hill near the pre-existing Kibuga (capital) of the Buganda Kingdom.
Regional roleCaptain Lugard would, later on, be allocated the Kampala hill that would soon be known as Old Kampala, and on which he built a fort.
Regional roleThe land in Buganda's Kibuga (capital), including Mengo Hill and Makerere Hill, was allocated to the young Kabaka, the Baganda colonial collaborators, etc., under mailo and freehold.

Stay overview

Base in Nakasero for an easy first stay near banks, markets and downtown offices, or choose Kololo for embassy-district calm and upscale hotels. Ntinda suits longer, quieter stays, while Kabalagala works for travelers prioritising nightlife.

Hotels & areas

Kampala hotel choice depends on priorities: Nakasero suits first-time visitors wanting central sights and markets, Kololo suits business and diplomatic travelers, and Ntinda or Kabalagala suit those wanting quieter residential streets or lively nightlife respectively.

Kololo

Kololo is an upscale hillside district of embassies and leafy streets, home to many of Kampala's higher-end hotels.

Travel tips

Best base

Choose the Kampala base by repeat journeys: sights, evening food, station access and the airport transfer should all still work.

Traffic planning

Build in extra time for Kampala's notorious traffic, especially during weekday rush hours around the city centre.

Airport transfers

Book a hotel transfer or taxi for the roughly hour-long drive from Entebbe International Airport, allowing extra time for traffic.

Boda-boda safety

Keep the Kampala plan compact: group nearby sights together and leave enough time for transfers, meals and weather changes.
