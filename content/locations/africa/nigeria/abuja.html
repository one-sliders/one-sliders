Abuja city overview

Abuja is Nigeria's purpose-built capital, planned in the 1980s around the dramatic granite outcrop of Aso Rock. First-time visitors should photograph Aso Rock from the Presidential Villa area, compare the Abuja National Mosque and National Christian Centre standing near each other, and stroll through Millennium Park's landscaped grounds. Nnamdi Azikiwe International Airport sits southeast of the city, and the striking Zuma Rock monolith makes an easy day trip north.

Regional role

In light of the ethnic and religious divisions of Nigeria, plans had been devised since Nigeria's independence to have its capital in a place deemed neutral to all major ethnic parties, and also near.

The move of Nigeria's Capital to Abuja was controversial, and the biggest opposition to it was led by Obafemi Awolowo.

Without the unstinting efforts of these experts, the momentum now exhibited at the beginning of the actual implementation of the new capital could not have been achieved.

The plan itself represents a milestone in the process of building the new capital city.

More detailed design of the central areas of the capital, particularly its monumental core and the Central Business District, was accomplished by Japanese architect Kenzo Tange with his team of city planners at Kenzo.

Most countries relocated their embassies to Abuja, and many maintain their former embassies as consulates in Lagos, the commercial capital of Nigeria.

Squatter settlements and towns have spread rapidly in and outside the city limits.

Stay overview

Maitama and Asokoro suit visitors wanting upscale hotels close to embassies and Aso Rock, while Wuse and Garki offer more central, mid-range options near shopping and business districts. Most of Abuja's main sights sit within a short drive of each other.

Travel tips

Best base

Choose Maitama or Asokoro for upscale hotels near embassies and Aso Rock, or Wuse for more central, better-value options.

See both faiths side by side

Visit the Abuja National Mosque and National Christian Centre together, since the two landmarks stand within sight of each other.

Day trip to Zuma Rock

Budget half a day for Zuma Rock, the giant granite monolith visible on the drive north from the city.

Traffic planning

Allow extra time for journeys during rush hour, since Abuja's wide roads can still see heavy congestion at peak times.
