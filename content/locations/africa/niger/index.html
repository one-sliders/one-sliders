Niger

History

14th-19th c.Agadez became a Tuareg sultanate and a trans-Saharan trade hub.
1922France made Niger a colony within French West Africa.
1960Independence came on 3 August.
1990sTuareg rebellions in the north ended in peace agreements.
2010A new constitution restored civilian rule after a coup.

Worth seeing

  • Air and Tenere: A desert reserve of massifs and dunes, a UNESCO World Heritage site.
  • Agadez: The mud-brick minaret of the 16th-century mosque, a UNESCO site.
  • W National Park: Savannah on the Niger River shared with Benin and Burkina Faso.
