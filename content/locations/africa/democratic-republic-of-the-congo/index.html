Democratic Republic of the Congo

History

14th-19th c.The Kingdom of Kongo and later Luba and Lunda states organised the region.
1885Leopold II of Belgium held the territory as a personal possession, the Congo Free State.
1908Belgium annexed the colony after international outcry over forced labour.
1960Independence came on 30 June, with Patrice Lumumba as first prime minister.
1965-1997Mobutu Sese Seko ruled, renaming the country Zaire.
1996-2003Two wars drew in neighbouring states and caused vast loss of life.

Worth seeing

  • Virunga National Park: Africa's oldest national park, with mountain gorillas and the Nyiragongo lava lake.
  • Congo River: The river route between Kinshasa and Kisangani, the country's historic artery.
  • Garamba National Park: Northern savannah reserve, a UNESCO World Heritage site.
